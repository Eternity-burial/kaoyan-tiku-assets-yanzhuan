# 英语一 2021年 小作文_应用文 (Part A)

> **分值**：10 分 | **字数要求**：100 词  

## 题目要求 (Directions)

A foreign friend of yours has recently graduated from college and
intends to find a job in China. Write him/her an email to make some
suggestions.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not use your own name in the email. Use " Li Ming"
instead. (10 points)
