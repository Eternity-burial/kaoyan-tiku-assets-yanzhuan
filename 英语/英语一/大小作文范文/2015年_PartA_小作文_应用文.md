# 英语一 2015年 小作文_应用文 (Part A)

> **分值**：10 分 | **字数要求**：100 词  

## 题目要求 (Directions)

You are going to host a club reading session. Write an email of about
100 words recommending a book to the club members.

You should state reasons for your recommendation.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use Li Ming
instead.

Do not write the address. (10 points)
