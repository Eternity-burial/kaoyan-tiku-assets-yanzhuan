## 二、 原文精读与逐句长难句语法树精析

### 【第 1 段】

#### 句子 `en1-2025-text3-s1`

> **英文原句**：Ever since taking on Netflix Inc. at its own game, old Hollywood has struggled to turn a profit in streaming, with the likes of Disney+, Peacock and Paramount+ losing billions of dollars each year, sparking concerns on Wall Street that the services will never be as profitable as cable once was.  
> **参考译文**：自从以奈飞公司自己的方式与其展开竞争以来，好莱坞的老牌制片公司一直难以在流媒体业务上盈利，迪士尼+、Peacock 和派拉蒙+等平台每年都亏损数十亿美元，从而在华尔街引发了担忧，担心这些流媒体服务永远无法像曾经的有线电视那样盈利。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：old Hollywood
- **谓语**：has struggled
- **宾语或表语**：to turn a profit in streaming
- **主干翻译**：好莱坞的老牌制片公司一直难以在流媒体业务上盈利。

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[非谓语作状语]** `Ever since taking on Netflix Inc. at its own game` (修饰: has struggled) — Ever since 引导的时间状语，taking on 为动名词，作时间状语，限定主句动作的起始时间。
- **[非谓语作状语]** `to turn a profit in streaming` (修饰: struggled) — 不定式作 struggled 的宾语/补足成分，表示努力的目标。
- **[非谓语作状语]** `with the likes of Disney+, Peacock and Paramount+ losing billions of dollars each year` (修饰: has struggled) — with 复合结构（with + 名词 + 现在分词）作伴随状语，补充说明亏损的具体情况。
- **[并列结构]** `Disney+, Peacock and Paramount+` (修饰: the likes) — 三个并列的流媒体平台，作 the likes of 的宾语。
- **[非谓语作状语]** `sparking concerns on Wall Street that the services will never be as profitable as cable once was` (修饰: has struggled) — 现在分词短语作结果状语，承接前文亏损这一事实所导致的后果。
- **[同位语从句]** `that the services will never be as profitable as cable once was` (修饰: concerns) — that 引导同位语从句，说明 concerns 的具体内容。
- **[状语从句(比较)]** `as cable once was` (修饰: profitable) — as...as 比较结构中的后一个 as 引导比较状语从句，省略了 profitable，与流媒体服务的盈利能力作对比。

#### 句子 `en1-2025-text3-s2`

> **英文原句**：But the age of streaming has been a boon for some unintended winners: pirates that use software to rip a film or television show in seconds from legitimate online video platforms and host the titles on their own, illegitimate services, which rake in about $2 billion annually from ads and subscriptions.  
> **参考译文**：但流媒体时代对一些意外的赢家来说却是一大福音：那些盗版者利用软件在数秒内从合法的在线视频平台扒取电影或电视节目，并将这些影视作品放到自己的非法服务平台上，而这些平台每年从广告和订阅中赚取约 20 亿美元。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：the age of streaming
- **谓语**：has been
- **宾语或表语**：a boon for some unintended winners
- **主干翻译**：流媒体时代对一些意外的赢家来说是一大福音。

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[插入语]** `pirates that use software to rip a film or television show in seconds from legitimate online video platforms and host the titles on their own, illegitimate services, which rake in about $2 billion annually from ads and subscriptions` (修饰: winners) — 冒号后的名词短语 pirates... 作 winners 的同位语，进一步说明意外赢家的身份。
- **[定语从句]** `that use software to rip a film or television show in seconds from legitimate online video platforms and host the titles on their own, illegitimate services` (修饰: pirates) — that 引导定语从句修饰 pirates，含两个并列谓语 use 和 host。
- **[非谓语作状语]** `to rip a film or television show in seconds from legitimate online video platforms` (修饰: use) — 不定式短语作 use software 的目的状语，表明使用软件的目的。
- **[并列结构]** `host the titles on their own, illegitimate services` (修饰: use) — 与 use 并列的第二个谓语动词 host，由 and 连接，同属定语从句。
- **[定语从句]** `which rake in about $2 billion annually from ads and subscriptions` (修饰: services) — which 引导非限制性定语从句，修饰 illegitimate services。

#### 句子 `en1-2025-text3-s3`

> **英文原句**：With no video production costs, illegal streaming sites have achieved profit margins approaching 90%, according to the Motion Picture Association (MPA), a trade group representing Hollywood studios that's working to crack down on the thousands of illegal platforms that have cropped up in recent years.  
> **参考译文**：据美国电影协会（MPA）称，由于没有视频制作成本，非法流媒体网站实现了接近 90% 的利润率。美国电影协会是一个代表好莱坞各制片公司的行业组织，正致力于打击近年来涌现的数千个非法平台。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：illegal streaming sites
- **谓语**：have achieved
- **宾语或表语**：profit margins
- **主干翻译**：非法流媒体网站实现了可观的利润率。

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[介词短语作状语]** `With no video production costs` (修饰: have achieved) — with 介词短语作原因状语，说明高利润率的原因。
- **[非谓语作定语]** `approaching 90%` (修饰: margins) — 现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 profit margins。
- **[介词短语作状语]** `according to the Motion Picture Association (MPA)` (修饰: have achieved) — according to 介词短语作状语，表明信息来源。
- **[插入语]** `a trade group representing Hollywood studios that's working to crack down on the thousands of illegal platforms that have cropped up in recent years` (修饰: Association) — 名词短语作 the Motion Picture Association 的同位语，补充说明其性质。
- **[非谓语作定语]** `representing Hollywood studios` (修饰: group) — 现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 a trade group。
- **[定语从句]** `that's working to crack down on the thousands of illegal platforms` (修饰: group) — that 引导定语从句修饰 a trade group，that's 即 that is。
- **[非谓语作状语]** `to crack down on the thousands of illegal platforms` (修饰: working) — 不定式短语作 working 的目的状语，表明该组织努力的目标。
- **[定语从句]** `that have cropped up in recent years` (修饰: thousands) — that 引导定语从句，修饰 the thousands of illegal platforms。
