### 【第 1 段】

#### 句子 `en2-2015-text3-s1`

> **英文原句**：Even in traditional offices, "the lingua franca of corporate America has gotten much more emotional and much more right-brained than it was 20 years ago," said Harvard Business School professor Nancy Koehn.  
> **参考译文**：哈佛商学院教授南希·科恩说："即使在传统的办公场所，美国企业界的通用语言也已变得比20年前更富情感、更偏感性了。"

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：Harvard Business School professor Nancy Koehn
- **谓语**：said
- **宾语或表语**："..."（所引述的话）
- **主干翻译**：哈佛商学院教授南希·科恩说道。

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[宾语从句]** `Even in traditional offices, "the lingua franca of corporate America has gotten much more emotional and much more right-brained than it was 20 years ago` (修饰: said) — 引号内整段直接引语作said的宾语。
- **[倒装]** `said Harvard Business School professor Nancy Koehn` (修饰: —) — 引语在前、说话人在后，谓语said提到主语之前的主谓倒装。
- **[介词短语作状语]** `Even in traditional offices` (修饰: has gotten) — 介词短语作地点状语，even强调让步。
- **[并列结构]** `much more right-brained` (修饰: much more emotional) — and连接两个much more引导的比较级表语。
- **[状语从句(比较)]** `than it was 20 years ago` (修饰: much more emotional and much more right-brained) — than引导比较状语从句，与more呼应。
