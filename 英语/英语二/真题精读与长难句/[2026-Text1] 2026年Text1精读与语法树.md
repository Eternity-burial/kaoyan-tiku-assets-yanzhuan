## 二、 原文精读与逐句长难句语法树精析

### 【第 1 段】

#### 句子 `en2-2026-text1-s1`

> **英文原句**：Ask people about public libraries and a certain image springs to mind: dusty, old-fashioned, the sort of place you enjoyed as a child but, rather like a British seaside town, would you go there now?  
> **参考译文**：问问人们对公共图书馆的看法，一个特定的形象就会浮现在脑海中：尘土飞扬、老式，那种你小时候喜欢的地方，但就像英国海滨小镇一样，你现在还会去吗？

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：a certain image
- **谓语**：springs
- **宾语或表语**：—
- **主干翻译**：一个特定的形象浮现

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[并列结构]** `Ask people about public libraries and a certain image springs to mind` (修饰: —) — 祈使句+and引导的并列句，表示条件与结果关系。
- **[插入语]** `dusty, old-fashioned, the sort of place you enjoyed as a child but, rather like a British seaside town, would you go there now?` (修饰: a certain image) — 冒号后为解释说明，其中包含并列形容词和名词短语，以及一个反问句。
- **[定语从句]** `you enjoyed as a child` (修饰: place) — 省略关系代词that的定语从句，修饰place。
- **[插入语]** `rather like a British seaside town` (修饰: would you go there now?) — 插入语，用于类比，说明反问句的语境。

### 【第 2 段】

#### 句子 `en2-2026-text1-s3`

> **英文原句**：The reality is startlingly different, as I discovered when the culture department commissioned me to conduct an independent review of English public libraries, published yesterday.  
> **参考译文**：现实情况却惊人地不同，正如我在文化部门委托我对英格兰公共图书馆进行独立审查时所发现的那样，该审查于昨天发布。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：The reality
- **谓语**：is
- **宾语或表语**：startlingly different
- **主干翻译**：现实情况惊人地不同

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[状语从句(方式)]** `as I discovered when the culture department commissioned me to conduct an independent review of English public libraries, published yesterday` (修饰: is) — as引导方式状语从句，表示‘正如’，从句中嵌套时间状语从句和定语从句。
- **[状语从句(时间)]** `when the culture department commissioned me to conduct an independent review of English public libraries, published yesterday` (修饰: discovered) — when引导时间状语从句，修饰discovered，说明发现的时间。
- **[非谓语作宾语补足语]** `to conduct an independent review of English public libraries, published yesterday` (修饰: me) — 不定式to conduct作宾语me的补足语，说明委托的内容。
- **[非谓语作定语]** `published yesterday` (修饰: review) — 过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰review，表示被动和完成。

> **英文原句**：As I visited libraries up and down the country, I was surprised to learn there are more than twice the number of libraries (2,892) as there are branches of McDonald's.

**【主干结构拆解】**：

- **谓语**：was surprised
- **宾语或表语**：—

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[状语从句(时间)]** `As I visited libraries up and down the country` (修饰: was surprised) — 由As引导的时间状语从句，表示主句动作发生的时间背景。
