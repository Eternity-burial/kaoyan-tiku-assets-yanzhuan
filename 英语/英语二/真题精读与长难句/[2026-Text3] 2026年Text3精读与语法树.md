## 一、 真题阅读理解题目与选项深度陷阱剖析

### 第 1 题：According to the first two paragraphs,the high-speed rail network in Italy .
**选项**：
- **A**. is seen by tourists as outdated 
- **B**. is a popular option for travelling  [★正确答案]
- **C**. is inadequately utilised by locals 
- **D**. is an example of good maintenance 

- **【官方标准答案】**：`B`
- **【解题思路精析】**：
〔2026 网传版解析，官方正式版出来后复核〕该题为细节题，问意大利高铁网在前两段呈现的整体特点是什 么。文中开头直接说高铁开通后，火车成为本地人和游客的首选(“trains have become the preferred means of travel … for locals and tourists alike.”)，并且很多时候是“最佳选择”(“In many cases, it is the best option ”)。因此它是大家常用、受欢迎的出行方式，选B。

---

### 第 2 题：Ferrovie is quoted in Paragraph 3 to showy .
**选项**：
- **A**. the common occurrence of train delays  [★正确答案]
- **B**. the value of the Italian train network 
- **C**. the wide media coverage of trains 
- **D**. the high efficiency of Trenitalia 

- **【官方标准答案】**：`A`
- **【解题思路精析】**：
〔2026 网传版解析，官方正式版出来后复核〕该题为例证题，问第3 段引用Ferrovie 的数据是为了说 明什么。第3 段先下结论，说晚点越来越常见(“Disruptions have become increasingly frequent… delays now a fixture …”)，紧接着用统计数据证明。数据的作用就是支撑“晚点很普遍”这一判断，所以选A。

### 第 3 题：Which of the following is one cause of the disruptions in the network?
**选项**：
- **A**. Complex train schedules 
- **B**. Shortage of investments 
- **C**. Its limited capacity  [★正确答案]
- **D**. Its massive scale 

- **【官方标准答案】**：`C`
- **【解题思路精析】**：
〔2026 网传版解析，官方正式版出来后复核〕该题为细节题，问造成铁路网络混乱/中断的原因之一是什么。第5 段首 句明确指出“运力不足”是另一个问题(“But lack of capacity is another problem.”)，并强调没有冗余容量 (“there is no spare capacity ”)，因此选C。

### 第 4 题：It can be learned that the mixed system .
**选项**：
- **A**. provides a wide choice of routes 
- **B**. requires extra operational spending 
- **C**. increases the level of travel comfort 
- **D**. puts the high-speed network in trouble  [★正确答案]

- **【官方标准答案】**：`D`
- **【解题思路精析】**：
〔2026 网传版解析，官方正式版出来后复核〕该题为细节题，问“混合系统（mixed system）”会带来什 么影响。原文解释混合系统：高铁在某些路段或穿过大城市时必须走普通轨道(“high-speed trains must travel on the regular tracks …”)；一旦普通线路拥堵或本地车故障，会波及整个高铁网(“If there’s congestion… the entire high-speed network is affected.”)。所以混合系统会让高铁网络陷入麻烦，选D。

**选项**：
- **A**. reconstructing the regular train lines 
- **B**. shortening high-speed train intervals  [★正确答案]
- **C**. building more stations in urban centres

- **【解题思路精析】**：

## 二、 原文精读与逐句长难句语法树精析

### 【第 1 段】

#### 句子 `en2-2026-text3-s1`

> **英文原句**：Since the 2008 launch of the high-speed rail network between Rome and Milan, trains have become the preferred means of travel across Italy for locals and tourists alike.  
> **参考译文**：自2008年罗马和米兰之间的高速铁路网开通以来，火车已成为意大利各地居民和游客首选的出行方式。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：trains
- **谓语**：have become
- **宾语或表语**：the preferred means of travel
- **主干翻译**：火车已成为首选的出行方式

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[介词短语作状语]** `Since the 2008 launch of the high-speed rail network between Rome and Milan` (修饰: have become) — 由Since引导的时间状语，说明主句动作发生的时间起点。
- **[介词短语作定语]** `across Italy` (修饰: travel) — 修饰travel，说明旅行的范围。
- **[介词短语作定语]** `for locals and tourists alike` (修饰: means of travel) — 修饰means of travel，说明适用对象。

#### 句子 `en2-2026-text3-s5`

- **谓语**：is
- **宾语或表语**：why the recent severe delays caused by numerous maintenance works have thrown the rail industry into chaos at a time when most people head on holiday, which matters for business in a country where tourism accounts for 10 percent of GDP
- **主干翻译**：这就是为什么……

**【成分与从句分析】**：

- **[定语从句]** `which matters for business in a country where tourism accounts for 10 percent of GDP` (修饰: the recent severe delays ... have thrown the rail industry into chaos) — which引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面整个主句的内容。
- **[定语从句]** `where tourism accounts for 10 percent of GDP` (修饰: country) — where引导定语从句修饰country，说明国家特点。
