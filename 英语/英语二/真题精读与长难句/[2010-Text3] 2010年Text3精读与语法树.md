## 二、 原文精读与逐句长难句语法树精析

### 【第 1 段】

#### 句子 `en2-2010-text3-s1`

> **英文原句**：Over the past decade, many companies had perfected the art of creating automatic behaviors – habits – among consumers.  
> **参考译文**：在过去十年里，许多公司已经完善了在消费者中培养自动行为（即习惯）的技艺。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：many companies
- **谓语**：had perfected
- **宾语或表语**：the art
- **主干翻译**：许多公司已经完善了这门技艺。

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[介词短语作状语]** `Over the past decade` (修饰: had perfected) — 时间状语，介词 over 引导，置于句首。
- **[介词短语作定语]** `of creating automatic behaviors – habits – among consumers` (修饰: the art) — of 介词短语作后置定语，其宾语为动名词 creating。
- **[同位语]** `habits` (修饰: automatic behaviors) — 破折号间的名词作同位语，解释 automatic behaviors。

### 【第 2 段】

#### 句子 `en2-2010-text3-s3`

> **英文原句**："There are fundamental public health problems, like dirty hands instead of a soap habit, that remain killers only because we can't figure out how to change people's habits," said Dr. Curtis, the director of the Hygiene Center at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine.  
> **参考译文**：“存在一些根本性的公共卫生问题，比如脏手而非用肥皂的习惯——它们之所以仍是致命杀手，仅仅是因为我们弄不清如何改变人们的习惯，”伦敦卫生与热带医学院卫生中心主任柯蒂斯博士说道。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：Dr. Curtis
- **谓语**：said
- **宾语或表语**："There are ... habits" (直接引语)
- **主干翻译**：柯蒂斯博士说……

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[倒装]** `said Dr. Curtis` (修饰: —) — 直接引语后引述部分主谓倒装。
- **[宾语从句]** `There are fundamental public health problems, like dirty hands instead of a soap habit, that remain killers only because we can't figure out how to change people's habits` (修饰: said) — 直接引语整体作 said 的宾语。
- **[插入语]** `like dirty hands instead of a soap habit` (修饰: problems) — like 引导的举例插入语，说明 problems。
- **[定语从句]** `that remain killers only because we can't figure out how to change people's habits` (修饰: problems) — that 引导限制性定语从句，修饰 problems。
- **[状语从句(原因)]** `only because we can't figure out how to change people's habits` (修饰: remain) — because 引导原因状语从句，only 加强。
- **[非谓语作宾语]** `how to change people's habits` (修饰: figure out) — 疑问词 how + 不定式构成短语作 figure out 的宾语。
- **[同位语]** `the director of the Hygiene Center at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine` (修饰: Dr. Curtis) — 名词短语作同位语，说明 Dr. Curtis 的身份。

> **英文原句**：If you look hard enough, you'll find that many of the products we use every day – chewing gums, skin moisturizers, disinfecting wipes, air fresheners, water purifiers, health snacks, teeth whiteners, fabric softeners, vitamins – are results of manufactured habits.  
> **参考译文**：如果你观察得足够仔细，就会发现我们每天使用的许多产品——口香糖、润肤乳、消毒湿巾、空气清新剂、净水器、健康零食、牙齿美白剂、衣物柔顺剂、维生素——都是「制造习惯」的产物。

**【主干结构拆解】**：
- **主语**：you
- **谓语**：'ll find
- **宾语或表语**：that …（宾语从句）
- **主干翻译**：你会发现……

**【成分与从句分析】**：
- **[状语从句(条件)]** `If you look hard enough` (修饰: 'll find) — if 引导条件状语从句。
- **[宾语从句]** `that many of the products we use every day – chewing gums, skin moisturizers, disinfecting wipes, air fresheners, water purifiers, health snacks, teeth whiteners, fabric softeners, vitamins – are results of manufactured habits` (修饰: 'll find) — that 引导宾语从句作 find 的宾语；从句主语 many of the products，谓语 are。
- **[定语从句]** `we use every day` (修饰: the products) — 省略关系代词 that/which 的限制性定语从句。
- **[同位语]** `chewing gums, skin moisturizers, disinfecting wipes, air fresheners, water purifiers, health snacks, teeth whiteners, fabric softeners, vitamins` (修饰: many of the products we use every day) — 两个破折号之间的名词列举，同位说明前面的 products。读的时候先跳过它，主干是 many of the products … are results of manufactured habits。
