# 英语二 2019年 小作文_应用文 (Part A)

> **分值**：10 分 | **字数要求**：100 词  

## 题目要求 (Directions)

Suppose Professor Smith asked you to plan a debate on the theme of city traffic.
Write him an email to
suggest a specific topic with your reasons, and
tell him about your arrangements.
You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your one name. Use "Li Ming" instead.
Do not write your address.
