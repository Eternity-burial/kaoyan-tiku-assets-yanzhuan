# 英语二 2026年 小作文_应用文 (Part A)

> **分值**：10 分 | **字数要求**：100 词  

## 题目要求 (Directions)

Suppose your friend Jack has shared with you a video of his family’s travel in China. Write an email to tell him
how you feel about the video, and
your wish to learn more about their travel.
Write you answer in about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.
Do not use your own name in your email; use “Li Ming” instead. (10 points)
