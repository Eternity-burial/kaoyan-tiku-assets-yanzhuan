### 第 1 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \( f(x) = \int_{0}^{x} (x - 2t) \mathrm{e}^{-t^2} dt (x \geqslant 0), g(x) = \int_{0}^{x} (x - 2t) \sin t^2 dt \)，则

- **(A)** \(f(x)\) 单调增加, \(g(x)\) 是奇函数.
- **(B)** \(f(x)\) 单调增加, \(g(x)\) 是偶函数.
- **(C)** \(f(x)\) 单调减少, \(g(x)\) 是奇函数.
- **(D)** \(f(x)\) 单调减少, \(g(x)\) 是偶函数.

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(f(x) = \int_{0}^{x}(x - 2t)\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t = x\int_{0}^{x}\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{x}2t\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t,\)
求导得
\[
f ^ {\prime} (x) = \int_ {0} ^ {x} \mathrm{e} ^ {- t ^ {2}} \mathrm{d} t + x \mathrm{e} ^ {- x ^ {2}} - 2 x \mathrm{e} ^ {- x ^ {2}} = \int_ {0} ^ {x} \mathrm{e} ^ {- t ^ {2}} \mathrm{d} t - x \mathrm{e} ^ {- x ^ {2}},
\]
易知 \( f'(0)=0 \) .
又 \(f''(x) = \mathrm{e}^{-x^2} - (\mathrm{e}^{-x^2} - 2x^2\mathrm{e}^{-x^2}) = 2x^2\mathrm{e}^{-x^2}\geqslant 0\) ，故 \(f^{\prime}(x)\) 单调增加，进而有 \(f^{\prime}(x)\geqslant f^{\prime}(0) = 0\) 则 \(f(x)\) 单调增加.
\[
g (x) = \int_ {0} ^ {x} (x - 2 t) \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t = x \int_ {0} ^ {x} \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t - \int_ {0} ^ {x} 2 t \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t,
\]
由于 \(\int_0^x\sin t^2\mathrm{d}t\) 为奇函数， \(\int_0^x 2t\sin t^2\mathrm{d}t\) 为偶函数，故 \(g(x)\) 为偶函数.
【注】对 \(g(x)\) 亦可求导，得
\[
g ^ {\prime} (x) = x \sin x ^ {2} + \int_ {0} ^ {x} \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t - 2 x \sin x ^ {2} = \int_ {0} ^ {x} \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t - x \sin x ^ {2},
\]
\[
g ^ {\prime \prime} (x) = \sin x ^ {2} - (\sin x ^ {2} + 2 x ^ {2} \cos x ^ {2}) = - 2 x ^ {2} \cos x ^ {2}.
\]
因  \( g''(x) \)  为偶函数, 且  \( g'(0)=0 \) , 故  \( g'(x) \)  为奇函数, 进而可知  \( g(x) \)  为偶函数.

</details>

### 第 2 题 (选择题 · 5分)

对于积分① \(\int_{0}^{2\pi} x \sin x \, \mathrm{d}x\)，② \(\int_{-2}^{2} x^3 2^x \, \mathrm{d}x\)，正确的说法是

- **(A)** ①为负值，②为正值.
- **(B)** ①为正值，②为负值.
- **(C)** ①②均为正值.
- **(D)** ①②均为负值.

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

对于①， \(\int_0^{2\pi}x\sin x\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^\pi x\sin x\mathrm{d}x + \int_\pi^{2\pi}x\sin x\mathrm{d}x\)
\[
= \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} x \sin x \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} (t + \pi) \sin (t + \pi) \mathrm{d} t
\]
\[
= \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} x \sin x \mathrm{d} x - \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} (x + \pi) \sin x \mathrm{d} x
\]
\[
= - \pi \int_ {0} ^ {\pi} \sin x \mathrm{d} x <   0.
\]
另外， \(\int_0^{2\pi}x\sin x\mathrm{d}x\) 也可直接计算.
\[
\begin{array}{l} \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} x \sin x \mathrm{d} x = - \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} x \mathrm{d} (\cos x) \\ = - x \cos x \Big | _ {0} ^ {2 \pi} + \int_ {0} ^ {2 \pi} \cos x d x \\ = - 2 \pi <   0. \\ \end{array}
\]
对于②，
\[
\begin{array}{l} \int_ {- 2} ^ {2} x ^ {3} 2 ^ {x} \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {- 2} ^ {0} x ^ {3} 2 ^ {x} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {2} x ^ {3} 2 ^ {x} \mathrm{d} x \\ = \int_ {2} ^ {0} t ^ {3} 2 ^ {- t} \mathrm{d} t + \int_ {0} ^ {2} x ^ {3} 2 ^ {x} \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {0} ^ {2} x ^ {3} (2 ^ {x} - 2 ^ {- x}) \mathrm{d} x > 0. \\ \end{array}
\]
故①为负值，②为正值.

</details>

### 第 3 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \( f'(x) > 0, f''(x) < 0, x > 0 \)，对任意的正整数 \( n \)，记
\[
I _ {1} = \frac {1}{2} [ f (n + 1) - f (n) ], \quad I _ {2} = f (n) - f \left(\frac {2 n - 1}{2}\right), \quad I _ {3} = f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) - f (n),
\]
则

- **(A)** \(I_{1} > I_{2} > I_{3}\)
- **(B)** \(I_{1} > I_{3} > I_{2}\).
- **(C)** \(I_{2} > I_{1} > I_{3}\).
- **(D)** \(I_{2} > I_{3} > I_{1}\).

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

由拉格朗日中值定理, 得
\[
I _ {2} = f (n) - f \left(\frac {2 n - 1}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{2} f ^ {\prime} (\xi), \frac {2 n - 1}{2} <   \xi <   n,
\]
\[
I _ {3} = f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) - f (n) = \frac {1}{2} f ^ {\prime} (\eta), n <   \eta <   \frac {2 n + 1}{2}.
\]
由 \( f''(x)<0 \) ，知 \( f'(x) \) 单调减少，故 \( f'(\xi)>f'(\eta) \) ，得 \( I_{2}>I_{3} \) .
又
\[
I _ {1} = \frac {1}{2} [ f (n + 1) - f (n) ] = \frac {1}{2} \left[ f (n + 1) - f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) \right] + \frac {1}{2} \left[ f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) - f (n) \right]
\]
\[
= \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\xi_ {1}) + \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\eta),
\]
其中， \(n < \eta < \frac{2n + 1}{2} < \xi_1 < n + 1\) ，故 \(f^{\prime}(\xi_1) < f^{\prime}(\eta)\) ，于是有
\[
I _ {1} <   \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\eta) + \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\eta) = \frac {1}{2} f ^ {\prime} (\eta) = I _ {3}.
\]
综上， \(I_{2} > I_{3} > I_{1}\)

</details>

### 第 4 题 (选择题 · 5分)

已知一阶差分方程 \(ay_{n+1} - \frac{1}{2} y_n = 1\) 的解 \(\{y_n\}\) 收敛, 则

- **(A)** \(a = \frac{1}{2}\).
- **(B)** \(a = -\frac{1}{2}\).
- **(C)** \(|a| < \frac{1}{2}\).
- **(D)** \(|a| > \frac{1}{2}\).

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

当 \(a = \frac{1}{2}\) 时，方程为 \(y_{n + 1} - y_n = 2\) ，齐次方程 \(y_{n + 1} - y_n = 0\) 的通解为 \(y_{\text{通}} = C \cdot 1^n = C\)
设  \( y^{*}=An \)  为方程  \( y_{n+1}-y_{n}=2 \)  的特解，则  \( A(n+1)-An=A=2 \) ，故方程  \( y_{n+1}-y_{n}=2 \)  的通解为  \( y_{n}=C+2n,\left\{y_{n}\right\} \)  发散。A 错误。
当 \(a = -\frac{1}{2}\) 时，方程为 \(y_{n + 1} + y_n = -2\) ，齐次方程 \(y_{n + 1} + y_n = 0\) 的通解为 \(y_{\text{通}} = C \cdot (-1)^n\)
设 \( y^{*} = A \) 为方程 \( y_{n+1} + y_n = -2 \) 的特解，则 \( 2A = -2 \)，得 \( A = -1 \)，故方程 \( y_{n+1} + y_n = -2 \) 的通解为 \( y_n = C \cdot (-1)^n - 1 \)，\( \{y_n\} \) 发散。B错误。
当 \(a = \frac{1}{4}\) 时，方程为 \(y_{n + 1} - 2y_n = 4\) ，齐次方程 \(y_{n + 1} - 2y_n = 0\) 的通解为 \(y_{\text{通}} = C \cdot 2^n\)
设 \( y^{*} = A \) 为方程 \( y_{n + 1} - 2y_{n} = 4 \) 的特解，则 \( A - 2A = 4 \)，得 \( A = -4 \)，故方程 \( y_{n + 1} - 2y_{n} = 4 \) 的通解为 \( y_{n} = C \cdot 2^{n} - 4 \)，\( \{y_{n}\} \) 发散.C错误.
事实上，方程可整理为 \( y_{n+1}-\frac{1}{2a}y_n=\frac{1}{a} \) ，对应的齐次差分方程的通解为 \( y_{\text{通}}=C\cdot\left(\frac{1}{2a}\right)^n \) .
当 \(a = \frac{1}{2}\) 时，由上述分析知，\(\{y_n\}\) 发散.
当 \(a \neq \frac{1}{2}\) 时，设 \(y^{*} = A\) 为方程的一个特解，则 \(A - \frac{1}{2a} A = \frac{1}{a}\)，得 \(A = \frac{1}{a} \cdot \frac{2a}{2a - 1} = \frac{2}{2a - 1}\)，故差分方程的通解为 \(y_{n} = C \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2a}\right)^{n} + \frac{2}{2a - 1}\)，若 \(\{y_{n}\}\) 收敛，则需满足 \(\left|\frac{1}{2a}\right| < 1\)，即 \(|a| > \frac{1}{2}\).

</details>

### 第 5 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(n\) 阶矩阵 \(A, B, C\) 满足 \(r(ABC) + 2n = r(A) + r(B) + r(C)\), 则

- **(A)** \(r(A) + r(B) + r(C) = r(AB)\).
- **(B)** \(r(AB) + n = r(A) + r(B)\).
- **(C)** \(r(ABC) > r(A) + r(BC) - n.\)
- **(D)** \(r(AB) = r(BC) = n.\)

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

因 \(r(ABC) \geqslant r(AB) + r(C) - n\)，则
\[
r (\boldsymbol {A}) + r (\boldsymbol {B}) + r (\boldsymbol {C}) = r (\boldsymbol {A B C}) + 2 n \geqslant r (\boldsymbol {A B}) + r (\boldsymbol {C}) - n + 2 n,
\]
即 \(r(A) + r(B) \geqslant r(AB) + n\).
又 \(r(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}) \geqslant r(\mathbf{A}) + r(\mathbf{B}) - n\)，即 \(r(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}) + n \geqslant r(\mathbf{A}) + r(\mathbf{B})\)。
综上， \(r(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{B}) + n = r(\mathbf{A}) + r(\mathbf{B})\) .故选项B正确.
同理,选项C不成立.
若 \(r(A) + r(B) + r(C) = r(AB)\)，则有 \(r(AB) = r(ABC) + 2n \geqslant 2n\)，矛盾，故选项A不成立。显然，选项D不一定成立。
【注】本题亦可举反例, 令 A = B = E, C = O, 可排除选项 A, C, D.

</details>

### 第 6 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设矩阵  \( \begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & a & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & b\end{pmatrix} \)  有 2 个正特征值和 1 个负特征值, 则

- **(A)** \(a > 4, b > 1\).
- **(B)** \(a <   4,b > 1\)
- **(C)** \((a - 4)b > 1\).
- **(D)** \((a - 4)b < 1\).

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

记矩阵 \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & a & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & b \end{pmatrix}\), 其为实对称矩阵, 则对应的二次型为
\[
\begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = x _ {1} ^ {2} + a x _ {2} ^ {2} + b x _ {3} ^ {2} + 4 x _ {1} x _ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} \\ = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + (a - 4) x _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} + b x _ {3} ^ {2}. \\ \end{array}
\]
①当 \(a = 4\) ， \(b\neq 0\) 时，
\[
\begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + b x _ {3} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} \\ = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + b \left(x _ {3} + \frac {1}{b} x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} - \frac {1}{b} x _ {2} ^ {2}, \\ \end{array}
\]
此时满足矩阵 A 的特征值两正一负的条件.
②当 \(a = 4\) ， \(b = 0\) 时，
\[
f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3}.
\]
令 \(\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{1} + 2x_{2} = y_{1}, \\ x_{2} = y_{2} + y_{3}, \end{array} \right.\) 得 \(f = y_{1}^{2} + 2y_{2}^{2} - 2y_{3}^{2}\)，此时满足矩阵 \(A\) 的特征值两正一负的条件.
\(x_{3} = y_{2} - y_{3}\)
③当 \(a \neq 4\) 时，
\[
\begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + (a - 4) x _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} + b x _ {3} ^ {2} \\ = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + (a - 4) \left(x _ {2} + \frac {1}{a - 4} x _ {3}\right) ^ {2} + \left(b - \frac {1}{a - 4}\right) x _ {3} ^ {2}. \\ \end{array}
\]
要满足矩阵 A 的特征值两正一负，则有  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}a-4&>0,\\ b-\frac{1}{a-4}&<0\end{aligned}\right. \)  或  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}a-4&<0,\\ b-\frac{1}{a-4}&>0,\end{aligned}\right. \)  此时均有  \( (a-4)b<1 \) .
综上， \((a - 4)b <   1\)

</details>

### 第 7 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设向量组 \(B: \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{r}\) 能由向量组 \(A: \alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{s}\) 线性表示为 \(\left( \begin{array}{c} \beta_{1} \\ \beta_{2} \\ \vdots \\ \beta_{n} \end{array} \right) = P \left( \begin{array}{c} \alpha_{1} \\ \alpha_{2} \\ \vdots \\ \alpha_{n} \end{array} \right)\), 其中 \(P\) 为 \(r \times s\) 矩
阵, 且向量组 \(A\) 线性无关, 则向量组 \(B\) 线性无关是 \(r(P) = r\) 的

- **(A)** 充分非必要条件.
- **(B)** 必要非充分条件.
- **(C)** 充分必要条件.
- **(D)** 既非充分也非必要条件.

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

必要性.用反证法证明.
假设  \( \beta_{1},\beta_{2},\cdots,\beta_{r} \)  线性相关，则存在不全为零的数  \( k_{1},k_{2},\cdots,k_{r} \) ，使
\[
k _ {1} \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {1} + k _ {2} \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {2} + \dots + k _ {r} \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {r} = \mathbf {0},
\]
则 \((k_{1},k_{2},\dots ,k_{r})\left( \begin{array}{c}\pmb{\beta}_{1}\\ \pmb{\beta}_{2}\\ \vdots \\ \pmb{\beta}_{r} \end{array} \right) = \mathbf{0}\)
即 \((k_{1}, k_{2}, \dots, k_{r}) \boldsymbol{P} \begin{pmatrix} \boldsymbol{a}_{1} \\ \boldsymbol{a}_{2} \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol{a}_{s} \end{pmatrix} = \mathbf{0}\).
由于  \( \alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{s} \)  线性无关, 故有
\[
(k _ {1}, k _ {2}, \dots , k _ {r}) \boldsymbol {P} = \mathbf {0}.
\]
设 \( p_{1},p_{2},\cdots,p_{r} \) 是P的行向量组,则有
\[
(k _ {1}, k _ {2}, \dots , k _ {r}) \left( \begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol {p} _ {1} \\ \boldsymbol {p} _ {2} \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol {p} _ {r} \end{array} \right) = \mathbf {0},
\]
即 \(k_{1}\pmb{p}_{1} + k_{2}\pmb{p}_{2} + \dots +k_{r}\pmb{p}_{r} = \mathbf{0},\)
其中  \( k_{1}, k_{2}, \cdots, k_{r} \)  不全为零，由此可得  \( p_{1}, p_{2}, \cdots, p_{r} \)  线性相关，从而  \( r(P) \neq r \) ，这与  \( r(P) = r \)  矛盾，假设错误，故  \( \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{r} \)  线性无关。必要性得证。
充分性.用反证法证明.
假设 \( r(\boldsymbol{P}) \neq r \)，则 \( \boldsymbol{P} \) 的 \( r \) 个行向量 \( \boldsymbol{p}_1, \boldsymbol{p}_2, \cdots, \boldsymbol{p}_r \) 线性相关，于是存在不全为零的数 \( k_1, k_2, \cdots, k_r \)，使
\[
k _ {1} \boldsymbol {p} _ {1} + k _ {2} \boldsymbol {p} _ {2} + \dots + k _ {r} \boldsymbol {p} _ {r} = \mathbf {0},
\]
即 \((k_{1}, k_{2}, \dots, k_{r})P = 0\)
分别在等式两端右边乘 \(\begin{pmatrix} a_1 \\ a_2 \\ \vdots \\ a_s \end{pmatrix}\), 有
\[
(k _ {1}, k _ {2}, \dots , k _ {r}) \boldsymbol {P} \left( \begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol {a} _ {1} \\ \boldsymbol {a} _ {2} \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol {a} _ {s} \end{array} \right) = \boldsymbol {0},
\]
于是
\[
(k _ {1}, k _ {2}, \dots , k _ {r}) \left( \begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {1} \\ \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {2} \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {r} \end{array} \right) = \mathbf {0},
\]
即 \(k_{1}\pmb{\beta}_{1} + k_{2}\pmb{\beta}_{2} + \dots +k_{r}\pmb{\beta}_{r} = \mathbf{0},\)
其中  \( k_{1}, k_{2}, \cdots, k_{r} \)  不全为零，于是  \( \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{r} \)  线性相关，这与  \( \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{r} \)  线性无关矛盾，假设错误，故  \( r(P) = r \)  。充分性得证。

</details>

### 第 8 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设随机变量 \(X\) 的概率密度为 \(f(x) = \frac{1}{2} \mathrm{e}^{-|x|}, x \in (-\infty, +\infty)\), 则

- **(A)** \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 不相关, \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 不相互独立.
- **(B)** \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 不相关, \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 相互独立.
- **(C)** \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 相关, \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 不相互独立.
- **(D)** \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 相关, \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 相互独立.

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

①由对称性,有
\[
E (X) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x f (x) \mathrm{d} x = 0, E (X | X |) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} x | x | f (x) \mathrm{d} x = 0,
\]
因此 \(\operatorname{Cov}(X, |X|) = E(X|X|) - E(X)E(|X|) = 0\)，知 \(X\) 与 \(|X|\) 不相关.
② \(P\{X \leqslant x_0\} = \int_{-\infty}^{x_0} f(x) \mathrm{d}x\)，取 \(x_0 = 1\)，则
\[
P \{X \leqslant 1 \} = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {1} \mathrm{e} ^ {- | x |} \mathrm{d} x = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {- \infty} ^ {0} \mathrm{e} ^ {x} \mathrm{d} x + \frac {1}{2} \int_ {0} ^ {1} \mathrm{e} ^ {- x} \mathrm{d} x = 1 - \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{e} ^ {- 1},
\]
\[
P \left\{\left| X \right| \leqslant 1 \right\} = \frac {1}{2} \int_ {1} ^ {1} \mathrm{e} ^ {- | x |} \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \mathrm{e} ^ {- x} \mathrm{d} x = 1 - \mathrm{e} ^ {- 1}.
\]
又事件 \(\{|X|\leqslant 1\} \subset \{X\leqslant 1\}\) ，即 \(\left\{|X|\leqslant 1\right\} \cap \left\{X\leqslant 1\right\} = \left\{|X|\leqslant 1\right\}\) ，故有
\[
