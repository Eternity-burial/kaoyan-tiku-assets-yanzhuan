### 第 1 题 (选择题 · 5分)

函数 \( f(x) = \frac{\left(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{x - 1}} - 1\right)|x|}{(x + 1)\ln |x - 1|} \) 的第一类间断点的个数为

- **(A)** 0.
- **(B)** 1.
- **(C)** 2.
- **(D)** 3.

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(f(x) = \frac{\left(\mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{x - 1}} - 1\right)|x|}{(x + 1)\ln |x - 1|}\) 的间断点为 \(x = -1,0,1,2\)
对于 \(x = -1\) ， \(\lim_{x\to -1}f(x) = \lim_{x\to -1}\frac{\mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{2}} - 1}{(x + 1)\ln 2} = \infty\) ，从而 \(x = -1\) 是无穷间断点.
对于 \(x = 0\) ， \(\lim_{x\to 0}f(x) = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{(\mathrm{e}^{-1} - 1)|x|}{\ln(1 - x)} = \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{(\mathrm{e}^{-1} - 1)|x|}{-x}\) ，显然 \(x = 0\) 是跳跃间断点.
对于 \(x = 1\) ， \(\lim_{x\to 1^{+}}f(x) = \lim_{x\to 1^{+}}\frac{\mathrm{e}^{\frac{1}{x - 1}} - 1}{2\ln(x - 1)} = \frac{u = \frac{1}{x - 1}}{u\to +\infty}\lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{\mathrm{e}^{u} - 1}{-2\ln u} = -\infty\) ，从而 \(x = 1\) 是无穷间断点.
对于 \(x = 2\) ， \(\lim_{x\to 2}f(x) = \lim_{x\to 2}\frac{2(\mathrm{e} - 1)}{3\ln(x - 1)} = \infty\) ，从而 \(x = 2\) 是无穷间断点.
故 \( f(x) \) 的第一类间断点的个数为1.

</details>

### 第 2 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(I_{1} = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{x}{\sqrt{1 - x^{2}}} \mathrm{d}x, I_{2} = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\cos x}{\sqrt{1 - x^{2}}} \mathrm{d}x, I_{3} = \int_{0}^{1} \frac{\sin x}{\sqrt{1 - x^{2}}} \mathrm{d}x\), 则

- **(A)** \(I_{1} <   I_{2} <   I_{3}\)
- **(B)** \(I_{2} <   I_{3} <   I_{1}\)
- **(C)** \(I_{3} <   I_{1} <   I_{2}\)
- **(D)** \(I_{2} <   I_{1} <   I_{3}\)

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

因为
\[
I _ {1} = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \frac {x}{\sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} \mathrm{d} x \frac {x = \sin t}{\int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \sin t \mathrm{d} t} = \int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \cos t \mathrm{d} t,
\]
\[
I _ {2} = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \frac {\cos x}{\sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} \mathrm{d} x \frac {x = \sin t}{\int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \cos (\sin t) \mathrm{d} t},
\]
\[
I _ {3} = \int_ {0} ^ {1} \frac {\sin x}{\sqrt {1 - x ^ {2}}} \mathrm{d} x \frac {x = \cos t}{\int_ {0} ^ {\frac {\pi}{2}} \sin (\cos t) \mathrm{d} t},
\]
且在 \(\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\) 上，
\[
0 <   \sin (\cos t) <   \cos t <   \cos (\sin t),
\]
所以 \(I_{3} < I_{1} < I_{2}\)

</details>

### 第 3 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(z = z(x, y)\) 是由方程 \(\int_{2x - 3y}^{z} f(2x - 3y + z - t) dt = \sin (2x - 3y + z)\) 所确定的函数, 其中 \(f\) 为大于 1 的连续函数, 则

- **(A)** \(3 \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + 2 \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0.\)
- **(B)** \(3 \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - 2 \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0.\)
- **(C)** \(2 \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + 3 \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0.\)
- **(D)** \(2 \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} - 3 \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0.\)

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

令 \(2x - 3y + z - t = u\)，则 \(\int_{2x - 3y}^{z} f(2x - 3y + z - t) \, \mathrm{d}t = \int_{2x - 3y}^{z} f(u) \, \mathrm{d}u\)。
再令 \(F(x,y,z) = \int_{2x - 3y}^{z}f(u)\mathrm{d}u - \sin (2x - 3y + z)\) ，则
\[
F _ {x} ^ {\prime} = - 2 f (2 x - 3 y) - 2 \cos (2 x - 3 y + z),
\]
\[
F _ {y} ^ {\prime} = 3 f (2 x - 3 y) + 3 \cos (2 x - 3 y + z),
\]
\[
F _ {z} ^ {\prime} = f (z) - \cos (2 x - 3 y + z).
\]
故 \(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{F_x'}{F_z'} = \frac{2[f(2x - 3y) + \cos(2x - 3y + z)]}{f(z) - \cos(2x - 3y + z)}\)
\[
\frac {\partial z}{\partial y} = - \frac {F _ {y} ^ {\prime}}{F _ {z} ^ {\prime}} = \frac {- 3 [ f (2 x - 3 y) + \cos (2 x - 3 y + z) ]}{f (z) - \cos (2 x - 3 y + z)}.
\]
于是， \(3\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} + 2\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = 0\)

</details>

### 第 4 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(a\) 与 \(b\) 都是常数, 若反常积分 \(\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^a (1 - e^{-x})}{(1 + x)^b} \mathrm{d}x\) 收敛, 则 \(a\) 与 \(b\) 的取值范围为

- **(A)** \(a < -2, b > a + 1\).
- **(B)** \(a < -2, b < a + 1\).
- **(C)** \(a > -2, b < a + 1\).
- **(D)** \(a > -2, b > a + 1\).

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(\int_0^{+\infty}\frac{x^a(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^b}\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^1\frac{x^a(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^b}\mathrm{d}x + \int_1^{+\infty}\frac{x^a(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^b}\mathrm{d}x = I_1 + I_2.\)
对于 \(I_{1} = \int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^{a}(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^{b}}\mathrm{d}x,\)
当 \(x \to 0^{+}\) 时，\(\frac{x^{a}(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^{b}} \sim x^{a + 1} = \frac{1}{x^{-(a + 1)}}\)，故等价于讨论 \(\int_{0}^{1} \frac{1}{x^{-(a + 1)}} \mathrm{d}x\) 的敛散性，则当 \(-(a + 1) < 1\)，即 \(a > -2\) 时，\(I_{1}\) 收敛；
对于 \(I_{2} = \int_{1}^{+\infty}\frac{x^{a}(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^{b}}\mathrm{d}x,\)
当 \(x \to +\infty\) 时, \(\frac{x^a(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^b} \sim x^{a - b} = \frac{1}{x^{b - a}}\), 故等价于讨论 \(\int_1^{+\infty} \frac{1}{x^{b - a}} \mathrm{d}x\) 的敛散性, 则当 \(b - a > 1\), 即 \(b > a + 1\) 时, \(I_2\) 收敛.
综上, 当 \(a > -2, b > a + 1\) 时, 反常积分 \(\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{x^a (1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})}{(1 + x)^b} \mathrm{d}x\) 收敛.

</details>

### 第 5 题 (选择题 · 5分)

已知 \(\alpha_{1} = (1,2)^{T}, \alpha_{2} = (a,1)^{T}, x = (x_{1},x_{2})^{T}\), 若二次型 \(f(x_{1},x_{2}) = \sum_{i=1}^{2}[a_{i},x]^{2}\) 正定, 其中 \([a_{i},x]\) 表示向量 \(a_{i}, x\) 的内积 (\(i=1,2\)), 则 \(a\) 的取值范围是

- **(A)** \(\left(-\infty, \frac{1}{2}\right) \cup \left(\frac{1}{2}, +\infty\right)\).
- **(B)** \(\left(-\infty, -\frac{1}{2}\right) \cup \left(-\frac{1}{2}, +\infty\right)\).
- **(C)** \(\left(-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)\).
- **(D)** \(\left\{\frac{1}{2}\right\}\).

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(f(x_{1},x_{2}) = [\alpha_{1},x]^{2} + [\alpha_{2},x]^{2}\)
\[
\begin{array}{l} = [ \boldsymbol {x}, \boldsymbol {a} _ {1} ] [ \boldsymbol {a} _ {1}, \boldsymbol {x} ] + [ \boldsymbol {x}, \boldsymbol {a} _ {2} ] [ \boldsymbol {a} _ {2}, \boldsymbol {x} ] \\ = \boldsymbol {x} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol {a} _ {1} \boldsymbol {a} _ {1} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol {x} + \boldsymbol {x} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol {a} _ {2} \boldsymbol {a} _ {2} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \boldsymbol {x} = \boldsymbol {x} ^ {\mathrm{T}} (\boldsymbol {a} _ {1} \boldsymbol {a} _ {1} ^ {\mathrm{T}} + \boldsymbol {a} _ {2} \boldsymbol {a} _ {2} ^ {\mathrm{T}}) \boldsymbol {x} \\ = \boldsymbol {x} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \left[ \binom {1} {2} (1, 2) + \binom {a} {1} (a, 1) \right] \boldsymbol {x} \\ = \boldsymbol {x} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \left[ \left( \begin{array}{c c} 1 & 2 \\ 2 & 4 \end{array} \right) + \left( \begin{array}{c c} a ^ {2} & a \\ a & 1 \end{array} \right) \right] \boldsymbol {x} \\ = \boldsymbol {x} ^ {\mathrm{T}} \left( \begin{array}{c c} 1 + a ^ {2} & 2 + a \\ 2 + a & 5 \end{array} \right) \boldsymbol {x}. \\ \end{array}
\]
若 \(f\) 正定，则 \(1 + a^2 > 0\) ， \(\left| \begin{array}{cc}1 + a^2 & 2 + a\\ 2 + a & 5 \end{array} \right| > 0\) ，即 \((2a - 1)^{2} > 0\) ，解得 \(a\neq \frac{1}{2}\)

</details>

### 第 6 题 (选择题 · 5分)

下列矩阵中, 可以经过若干次初等行变换得到矩阵 \(\left( \begin{array}{lll}1 & 1 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 0\\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right)\) 的是

- **(B)** \(\left( \begin{array}{lll}1 & 1 & 0 & 1\\ 1 & 1 & 2 & 5\\ 1 & 1 & 1 & 3 \end{array} \right).\)
- **(C)** \(\left( \begin{array}{lll}1 & 0 & 0 & 1\\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 3\\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 0 \end{array} \right).\)
- **(D)** \(\left( \begin{array}{lll}1 & 1 & 0 & 1\\ 1 & 2 & 0 & 1\\ 2 & 3 & 0 & 2 \end{array} \right).\)

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

### 第 7 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(A\) 为 2 阶方阵, \(\alpha\) 为 2 维非零列向量, 且 \(\alpha\) 不是 \(A\) 的特征向量, \(P = (\alpha, A\alpha), A^2\alpha + A\alpha - 2\alpha = 0\), 若矩阵 \(B\) 满足 \(AP = PB\), 则 \(B =\)

- **(A)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc} - 1 & 0\\ 1 & 2 \end{array} \right).\)
- **(B)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}0 & 1\\ 1 & -1 \end{array} \right)\)
- **(C)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc} - 1 & 1\\ 0 & 2 \end{array} \right).\)
- **(D)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}0 & 2\\ 1 & -1 \end{array} \right).\)

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

因为 \(A^2 a + Aa - 2a = 0\) ，所以 \(A^2 a = 2a - Aa\)
由 \(AP = PB\) ，得
\[
A (a, A a) = (A a, A ^ {2} a) = (a, A a) \left( \begin{array}{c c} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & - 1 \end{array} \right) = P B,
\]
故 \(B = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}\).
【注】若 \(P = (\alpha, A\alpha)\) 不可逆，则存在常数 \(k\) ，使得 \(A\alpha = k\alpha\) ，与 \(\alpha\) 不是 \(A\) 的特征向量矛盾，故 \(P\) 可逆.

</details>

### 第 8 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设随机变量 \(X\) 服从参数为 \(\lambda (\lambda > 0)\) 的指数分布, 若 \(P\{X > D(X)\} = [P\{X > E(X)\}]^3\), 则 \(\lambda =\)

- **(A)** 3.
- **(B)** \(\frac{1}{3}\).
- **(C)** \(\frac{2}{3}\).
- **(D)** \(\frac{3}{2}\).

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

因为 \(X \sim E(\lambda)\), 所以 \(X\) 的概率密度为 \(f(x) = \begin{cases} \lambda e^{-\lambda x}, & x > 0, \\ 0, & x \leqslant 0, \end{cases}\) 且 \(E(X) = \frac{1}{\lambda}, D(X) = \frac{1}{\lambda^2}\). 于是
\[
P \left\{X > D (X) \right\} = P \left\{X > \frac {1}{\lambda^ {2}} \right\} = \int_ {\frac {1}{\lambda^ {2}}} ^ {+ \infty} \lambda \mathrm{e} ^ {- \lambda x} \mathrm{d} x = \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{\lambda}},
\]
\[
P \left\{X > E (X) \right\} = P \left\{X > \frac {1}{\lambda} \right\} = \int_ {\frac {1}{\lambda}} ^ {+ \infty} \lambda \mathrm{e} ^ {- \lambda x} \mathrm{d} x = \mathrm{e} ^ {- 1}.
\]
由于 \(P\{X > D(X)\} = \left[P\{X > E(X)\}\right]^3\) ，故 \(\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{\lambda}} = \mathrm{e}^{-3}\) ，即 \(\lambda = \frac{1}{3}\)

</details>

### 第 9 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设随机变量 X 与 Y 相互独立,  \( D(X) \cdot D(Y) \neq 0 \) , 以下结论:
①  \( 3X + 1 \)  与 4Y - 2 相关;
② \(X + Y\) 与 \(X - Y\) 不相关;
③ \(X + Y\) 与 \(2Y + 1\) 相互独立;
④ \( e^{X} \) 与 \( 2Y+1 \) 相互独立.正确结论的个数为

- **(A)** 1.
- **(B)** 2.
- **(C)** 3.
- **(D)** 4.

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

由相互独立的性质, 知④正确.
对于①, 由 \(X, Y\) 相互独立, 有 \(\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) = 0\). 于是 \(\operatorname{Cov}(3X + 1, 4Y - 2) = 12\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) = 0\), ①错误.
对于②， \(\operatorname {Cov}(X + Y,X - Y) = \operatorname {Cov}(X,X) - \operatorname {Cov}(X,Y) + \operatorname {Cov}(Y,X) - \operatorname {Cov}(Y,Y)\)
\[
= D (X) - D (Y) \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} = 0, D (X) = D (Y), \\ \neq 0, D (X) \neq D (Y), \end{array} \right.
\]
②错误.
对于③, \(\operatorname{Cov}(X + Y, 2Y + 1) = 2\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y) + 2\operatorname{Cov}(Y, Y) = 2D(Y) \neq 0\), 故 \(X + Y\) 与 \(2Y + 1\) 相关, 则一定不相互独立, ③错误.

</details>

### 第 12 题 (填空题 · 5分)

微分方程 \(3(x + y - 1)\mathrm{d}x + (x + y)\mathrm{d}y = 0\) 的通解为 ____.

> **标准答案**：`\(\frac{1}{2} (3x + y) + \frac{3}{4}\ln |2x + 2y - 3| = C\) ( \(C\) 为任意常数)`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(\frac{\mathrm{dy}}{\mathrm{dx}} = \frac{3[1 - (x + y)]}{x + y}\)，令 \(u = x + y\)，则 \(\frac{\mathrm{du}}{\mathrm{dx}} = 1 + \frac{\mathrm{dy}}{\mathrm{dx}}\)，于是 \(\frac{\mathrm{du}}{\mathrm{dx}} - 1 = \frac{3(1 - u)}{u}\)，即 \(\frac{\mathrm{du}}{\mathrm{dx}} = \frac{3 - 2u}{u}\)，
分离变量并积分,得
\[
\int \frac {u}{2 u - 3} \mathrm{d} u = - \int \mathrm{d} x,
\]
恒等变形,有  \( \frac{1}{2}\int\frac{2u-3+3}{2u-3}du=-\int dx \) , 则
\[
\frac {1}{2} u + \frac {3}{4} \ln | 2 u - 3 | = - x + C.
\]
将 \(u = x + y\) 代入上式，得原微分方程的通解为
\[
\frac {1}{2} (x + y) + \frac {3}{4} \ln | 2 x + 2 y - 3 | = - x + C,
\]
即 \(\frac{1}{2} (3x + y) + \frac{3}{4}\ln |2x + 2y - 3| = C\) （ \(C\) 为任意常数）.

</details>

### 第 13 题 (填空题 · 5分)

设连续函数 \( f(x) \) 满足 \( f(x + 1) - f(x) = x^2, \int_0^1 f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x = 0 \)，则 \( \int_1^2 f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x = \) ____.
