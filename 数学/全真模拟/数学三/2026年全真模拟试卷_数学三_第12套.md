### 第 1 题 (选择题 · 5分)

\( f(x,y)=x^{4}+y^{4}-(x+y)^{2} \) 有

- **(A)** 2 个极小值点, 1 个极大值点.
- **(B)** 1 个极小值点, 1 个极大值点.
- **(C)** 3 个极小值点,无极大值点.
- **(D)** 2 个极小值点,无极大值点.

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

令  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}f_{x}^{\prime}&=4x^{3}-2x-2y=0,\\ f_{y}^{\prime}&=4y^{3}-2x-2y=0,\end{aligned}\right. \)
得到函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的3个驻点(0,0),(1,1),(-1,-1).求二阶偏导数,得
\[
f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) = 1 2 x ^ {2} - 2, f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) = - 2, f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) = 1 2 y ^ {2} - 2.
\]
在点(1,1)处，
\[
A = f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) = 1 0, B = f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) = - 2, C = f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) = 1 0,
\]
则 \(AC - B^{2} > 0, A > 0\)
故(1,1)是函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的极小值点；
在点 \( (-1,-1) \) 处，
\[
A = f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (- 1, - 1) = 1 0, B = f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (- 1, - 1) = - 2, C = f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (- 1, - 1) = 1 0,
\]
则 \(AC - B^{2} > 0, A > 0\)
故 \( (-1,-1) \) 是函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的极小值点；
在点 \( (0,0) \) 处，
\[
A = f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (0, 0) = - 2, B = f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (0, 0) = - 2, C = f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (0, 0) = - 2,
\]
则 \(AC - B^{2} = 0\)
无法使用充分条件判断. 对于 \(0 < |x| < 1\), 有
\[
f (x, 0) = x ^ {4} - x ^ {2} <   0, f (x, - x) = 2 x ^ {4} > 0, f (0, 0) = 0,
\]
故 \( (0,0) \) 不是函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的极值点.故选D.

</details>

### 第 3 题 (选择题 · 5分)

若级数 \(\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} (-1)^{n+1} \frac{\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}}{n^p}\) 条件收敛, 则 \(p\) 的取值范围为

- **(A)** \(\left(-1, \frac{1}{2}\right]\).
- **(B)** \((-1, 1)\).
- **(C)** \((0,1)\).
- **(D)** \( \left(-\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right]. \)

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

令 \(a_{n} = \frac{\sqrt{n + 1} - \sqrt{n}}{n^{p}} >0\) ，则当 \(n\to \infty\) 时，
\[
a _ {n} = \frac {1}{(\sqrt {n + 1} + \sqrt {n}) n ^ {p}} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {n} \left(\sqrt {1 + \frac {1}{n}} + 1\right) n ^ {p}} \sim \frac {1}{2 n ^ {\frac {1}{p + \frac {1}{2}}}}.
\]
故当 \( p+\frac{1}{2}>1 \) ，即 \( p>\frac{1}{2} \) 时， \( \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{n} \) 收敛，则原级数绝对收敛；
当 \( p+\frac{1}{2}\leqslant1 \) ，即 \( p\leqslant\frac{1}{2} \) 时， \( \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_{n} \) 发散，则原级数非绝对收敛.
当 \(0 < p + \frac{1}{2} \leqslant 1\)，即 \(-\frac{1}{2} < p \leqslant \frac{1}{2}\) 时，显然 \(a_{n} \to 0 (n \to \infty)\). 令
\[
f (x) = x ^ {p} (\sqrt {x + 1} + \sqrt {x}) (x > 0),
\]
由于 \(f^{\prime}(x) = x^{p - 1}(\sqrt{x + 1} +\sqrt{x})\left(p + \frac{\sqrt{x}}{2\sqrt{x + 1}}\right),\)
且 \(x^{p - 1} > 0,\sqrt{x + 1} +\sqrt{x} >0\)
而 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}\left(p + \frac{\sqrt{x}}{2\sqrt{x + 1}}\right) = p + \frac{1}{2} >0,\)
所以当 \(x\) 充分大时, \(f(x)\) 单调增加, 于是当 \(n\) 充分大时, \(a_{n} = \frac{1}{f(n)}\) 单调减少, 故由莱布尼茨判别法可知, 当 \(-\frac{1}{2} < p \leqslant \frac{1}{2}\) 时, 原级数条件收敛;
当 \( p+\frac{1}{2}\leqslant0 \) ，即 \( p\leqslant-\frac{1}{2} \) 时， \( a_{n} \) 不趋于 \( 0(n\to\infty) \) ，故当 \( p\leqslant-\frac{1}{2} \) 时，原级数发散。故选D.

</details>

### 第 4 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设函数 \( f(t) \) 连续, 令 \( F(x, y) = \int_{0}^{y - x} (y - x - t)f(t)dt \), 则

- **(A)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).
- **(B)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = -\frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).
- **(C)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).
- **(D)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = -\frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(F(x,y) = y\int_{0}^{y - x}f(t)\mathrm{d}t - x\int_{0}^{y - x}f(t)\mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{y - x}tf(t)\mathrm{d}t,\)
则
\[
\frac {\partial F}{\partial x} = - y f (y - x) - \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t + x f (y - x) + (y - x) f (y - x) = - \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t,
\]
\[
\frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial x ^ {2}} = f (y - x),
\]
\[
\frac {\partial F}{\partial y} = \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t + y f (y - x) - x f (y - x) - (y - x) f (y - x) = \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t,
\]
\[
\frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial y ^ {2}} = f (y - x),
\]
可得
\[
\frac {\partial F}{\partial x} = - \frac {\partial F}{\partial y} \cdot \frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial x ^ {2}} = \frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial y ^ {2}}.
\]
故选C.

</details>

### 第 5 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(A, B\) 是 \(n\) 阶矩阵, \(A^{*}, B^{*}\) 分别是 \(A, B\) 对应的伴随矩阵, 则分块矩阵 \(C = \begin{pmatrix} O & A \\ B & O \end{pmatrix}\) 的伴随矩阵 \(C^{*} =\)

- **(A)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}O & |A|A^{*}\\ |B|B^{*} & O \end{array} \right).\)
- **(B)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}O & (-1)^{n}|A|B^{*}\\ (-1)^{n}|B|A^{*} & O \end{array} \right).\)
- **(C)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}O & |B|A^{*}\\ |A|B^{*} & O \end{array} \right).\)
- **(D)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}O & (-1)^{n}|B|B^{*}\\ (-1)^{n}|A|A^{*} & O \end{array} \right).\)

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

代入选项逐个验算即可,其中
\[
\begin{array}{l} \left( \begin{array}{c c} \boldsymbol {O} & \boldsymbol {A} \\ \boldsymbol {B} & \boldsymbol {O} \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c} \boldsymbol {O} & (- 1) ^ {n} | \boldsymbol {A} | \boldsymbol {B} ^ {*} \\ (- 1) ^ {n} | \boldsymbol {B} | \boldsymbol {A} ^ {*} & \boldsymbol {O} \end{array} \right) \\ = \left( \begin{array}{c c} (- 1) ^ {n} | \boldsymbol {B} | \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {A} ^ {*} & \boldsymbol {O} \\ \boldsymbol {O} & (- 1) ^ {n} | \boldsymbol {A} | \boldsymbol {B} \boldsymbol {B} ^ {*} \end{array} \right) \\ = (- 1) ^ {n} | \boldsymbol {A} | | \boldsymbol {B} | \left( \begin{array}{c c} \boldsymbol {E} & \boldsymbol {O} \\ \boldsymbol {O} & \boldsymbol {E} \end{array} \right) = | \boldsymbol {C} | \left( \begin{array}{c c} \boldsymbol {E} & \boldsymbol {O} \\ \boldsymbol {O} & \boldsymbol {E} \end{array} \right) = \boldsymbol {C C} ^ {*}. \\ \end{array}
\]
故选B.

### 第 6 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设矩阵 \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 1 \\ -2 & 2 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 & k \end{pmatrix}, r((3E - A)^2) < r(3E - A)\), 其中 \(E\) 是 3 阶单位矩阵, 则常数 \(k =\)

- **(A)** 3.
- **(B)** 4.
- **(C)** 5.
- **(D)** 6.

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

直接计算, 得
\[
3 \boldsymbol {E} - \boldsymbol {A} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 2 & 1 & - 1 \\ 2 & 1 & - 1 \\ - 1 & 1 & 3 - k \end{array} \right),
\]
\[
(3 \boldsymbol {E} - \boldsymbol {A}) ^ {2} = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 2 & 1 & - 1 \\ 2 & 1 & - 1 \\ - 1 & 1 & 3 - k \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 2 & 1 & - 1 \\ 2 & 1 & - 1 \\ - 1 & 1 & 3 - k \end{array} \right) = \left( \begin{array}{c c c} 7 & 2 & k - 6 \\ 7 & 2 & k - 6 \\ k - 3 & 3 - k & (3 - k) ^ {2} \end{array} \right).
\]
因为无论 k 取何值, 都有  \( r(3E-A)=2 \) , 所以当且仅当 k=3 时,  \( r((3E-A)^{2})=1 \) , 满足  \( r((3E-A)^{2})<r(3E-A) \) . 因此, k=3. 故选 A.

### 第 7 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(n\) 阶方阵 \(A = (\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n}), B = (\beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{n}), AB = (\gamma_{1}, \gamma_{2}, \cdots, \gamma_{n})\), 记向量组 \(I: \alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{n}\), 向量组 \(II: \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{n}\), 向量组 \(III: \gamma_{1}, \gamma_{2}, \cdots, \gamma_{n}\). 如果向量组 \(III\) 线性相关, 则

- **(A)** 向量组 \(I\) 线性相关.
- **(B)** 向量组 \(II\) 线性相关.
- **(C)** 向量组 \(I\) 与 \(II\) 都线性相关.
- **(D)** 向量组 \(I\) 与 \(II\) 至少有一个线性相关.

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

### 第 8 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设随机变量 \((X_{1}, X_{2}) \sim N(0, 0; \sigma_{1}^{2}, \sigma_{2}^{2}; \rho), \sigma_{1} \neq \sigma_{2}\), 若 \(Y_{1} = X_{1} \cos \alpha + X_{2} \sin \alpha\) 与 \(Y_{2} = X_{2} \cos \alpha - X_{1} \sin \alpha\) 相互独立, \(\cos 2\alpha \neq 0\), 则 \(\tan 2\alpha =\)

- **(A)** \(\rho \frac{\sigma_1^2\sigma_2^2}{\sigma_1^2 - \sigma_2^2}\).
- **(B)** \(\rho \frac{\sigma_1^2\sigma_2^2}{\sigma_2^2 - \sigma_1^2}\)
- **(C)** \(2 \rho \frac{\sigma_1 \sigma_2}{\sigma_1^2 - \sigma_2^2}\).
- **(D)** \(2 \rho \frac{\sigma_1 \sigma_2}{\sigma_2^2 - \sigma_1^2}\).

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2) = \operatorname{Cov}(X_1\cos \alpha +X_2\sin \alpha ,X_1(-\sin \alpha) + X_2\cos \alpha)\)
\[
= \cos \alpha \cdot (- \sin \alpha) \cdot D (X _ {1}) + (\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha) \operatorname{Cov} (X _ {1}, X _ {2}) + \cos \alpha \cdot \sin \alpha \cdot D (X _ {2})
\]
\[
= \cos \alpha \cdot \sin \alpha \left(\sigma_ {2} ^ {2} - \sigma_ {1} ^ {2}\right) + \rho \sigma_ {1} \sigma_ {2} \left(\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha\right)
\]
\[
= \frac {1}{2} \left(\sigma_ {2} ^ {2} - \sigma_ {1} ^ {2}\right) \sin 2 \alpha + \rho \sigma_ {1} \sigma_ {2} \cos 2 \alpha
\]
\[
= \cos 2 \alpha \left[ \frac {1}{2} \left(\sigma_ {2} ^ {2} - \sigma_ {1} ^ {2}\right) \tan 2 \alpha + \rho \sigma_ {1} \sigma_ {2} \right] = 0.
\]
由 \(\cos 2\alpha \neq 0\) ，有 \(\frac{1}{2} (\sigma_2^2 -\sigma_1^2)\tan 2\alpha = -\rho \sigma_1\sigma_2\) ，即 \(\tan 2\alpha = 2\rho \frac{\sigma_1\sigma_2}{\sigma_1^2 - \sigma_2^2}\) ，选C.

</details>

### 第 9 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(X_{1}, X_{2}\) 是取自正态总体 \(X \sim N(1, 1)\) 的简单随机样本, 则 \(\frac{X_{1} - 1}{|1 - X_{2}|}\) 服从

- **(A)** \(t(1)\).
- **(B)** \(F(1,1)\).
- **(C)** \(\chi^2 (1)\)
- **(D)** \(N(1,1)\).

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

由题可知， \( X_{1} \) ， \( X_{2} \) 相互独立，故由t分布形式可知
\[
\frac {X _ {1} - 1}{| 1 - X _ {2} |} = \frac {\frac {X _ {1} - 1}{1}}{\sqrt {\left(\frac {X _ {2} - 1}{1}\right) ^ {2}} / 1} \sim t (1).
\]
故选A.

</details>

### 第 11 题 (填空题 · 5分)

设 \(x \to 0\) 时, \(\ln \frac{2 + x}{2 + \sin x}\) 与 \(x^n\) 为同阶无穷小, 则 \(n =\) ____.

> **标准答案**：`3`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

当 \(x \to 0\) 时，
\[
\ln \frac {2 + x}{2 + \sin x} = \ln \frac {2 + \sin x - \sin x + x}{2 + \sin x} \sim \frac {- \sin x + x}{2 + \sin x} \sim \frac {1}{2} \left(\frac {1}{6} x ^ {3}\right) = \frac {1}{1 2} x ^ {3}.
\]
故与 \( x^{3} \) 同阶，n=3。

</details>

### 第 12 题 (填空题 · 5分)

设 \( y \geqslant -2 \)，则微分方程 \( x' - \sqrt{x + y^2} = -2y \) 满足 \( x(0) = 1 \) 的特解为 ____.

> **标准答案**：`\( x=-\frac{3}{4}y^{2}+y+1(y\geqslant-2) \)`  

<details>

设随机变量 \(X\) 满足 \(|X| \leqslant 1, P\{X = -1\} = \frac{1}{4}, P\{X = 1\} = \frac{1}{2}\), 当 \(|X| < 1\) 时, \(X\) 服从 \((-1, 1)\) 上的均匀分布, 与 \(X\) 相互独立的随机变量 \(Y\) 服从参数为 1 的指数分布, 令 \(Z = X + Y\). 求
(1) \(X\) 的分布函数 \(F_{X}(x)\);
(2) \(Z\) 的分布函数 \(F_{Z}(z)\).

（1）当 \(x < -1\) 时，\(F_{X}(x) = P\{X \leqslant x\} = 0\)；
当 \(x = -1\) 时，\(F_{X}(x) = P\{X \leqslant x\} = \frac{1}{4}\);
当-1<x<1时，
\[
F _ {X} (x) = P \{X \leqslant - 1 \} + P \{- 1 <   X \leqslant x \},
\]
其中 \(P\{-1 < X \leqslant x\} = P\{-1 < X \leqslant x, \Omega\}\)
\[
= P \{- 1 <   X \leqslant x, - 1 <   X <   1 \} + P \{- 1 <   X \leqslant x, - 1 <   X <   1 \}
\]
\[
= P \{- 1 <   X \leqslant x \mid - 1 <   X <   1 \} P \{- 1 <   X <   1 \} + P \{- 1 <   X \leqslant x \mid \overline {{- 1 <   X <   1}} \} P \{- 1 <   X <   1 \}
\]
\[
= \frac {x + 1}{1 - (- 1)} \cdot \frac {1}{4} + 0 = \frac {x + 1}{8},
\]
故 \( F_{X}(x)=\frac{1}{4}+\frac{x+1}{8}=\frac{3+x}{8} \) ;
当 \( x\geqslant1 \) 时， \( F_{X}(x)=1 \) .
