### 第 1 题 (选择题 · 5分)

当 \(t \to 0^{+}\) 时, \(\alpha(t) = \int_{0}^{t} dx \int_{x}^{t} \sin(xy)^{2} \mathrm{d}y\) 是关于 \(t\) 的 \(n\) 阶无穷小, 则 \(n =\)

- **(A)** 3.
- **(B)** 4.
- **(C)** 5.
- **(D)** 6.

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

交换积分顺序,有
\[
\alpha (t) = \int_ {0} ^ {t} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {x} ^ {t} \sin (x y) ^ {2} \mathrm{d} y = \int_ {0} ^ {t} \mathrm{d} y \int_ {0} ^ {y} \sin (x y) ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x
\]
由洛必达法则,有 \( \lim_{t\to0^{+}}\frac{\alpha(t)}{t^{n}}=\lim_{t\to0^{+}}\frac{\int_{0}^{t}\sin(tx)^{2}dx}{nt^{n-1}}. \)
又 \(\int_0^t\sin (tx)^2\mathrm{d}x\xrightarrow{tx = u}\frac{1}{t}\int_0^{t^2}\sin u^2\mathrm{d}u(t\neq 0)\) ，故
\[
\lim _ {t \rightarrow 0 ^ {+}} \frac {\int_ {0} ^ {t} \sin (t x) ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x}{n t ^ {n - 1}} = \lim _ {t \rightarrow 0 ^ {+}} \frac {\int_ {0} ^ {t ^ {2}} \sin u ^ {2} \mathrm{d} u}{n t ^ {n}} = \lim _ {t \rightarrow 0 ^ {+}} \frac {2 t \sin t ^ {4}}{n ^ {2} t ^ {n - 1}} = \lim _ {t \rightarrow 0 ^ {+}} \frac {2 t ^ {5}}{n ^ {2} t ^ {n - 1}}
\]
故 \(n - 1 = 5, n = 6\). D 正确.

</details>

### 第 2 题 (选择题 · 5分)

曲线 \( y = \frac{x^2 + 1}{\sqrt{x^2 - 1}} \) 的渐近线的条数为

- **(A)** 1.
- **(B)** 2.
- **(C)** 3.
- **(D)** 4.

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

由 \(\lim_{x\to 1^{+}}\frac{x^2 + 1}{\sqrt{x^2 - 1}} = +\infty\) ， \(\lim_{x\to (-1)^{-}}\frac{x^2 + 1}{\sqrt{x^2 - 1}} = +\infty\) ，知 \(x = 1,x = -1\) 是两条铅直渐近线，
由 \(k_{1} = \lim_{x\to -\infty}\frac{y}{x} = \lim_{x\to -\infty}\frac{x^{2} + 1}{x\sqrt{x^{2} - 1}} = -\lim_{x\to -\infty}\frac{1 + \frac{1}{x^{2}}}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{1}{x^{2}}}} = -1\)
\[
b _ {1} = \lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} (y - k _ {1} x) = \lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} \left(\frac {x ^ {2} + 1}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - 1}} + x\right) = 0
\]
知 \(y = -x\) 为斜渐近线.
同理可求得 \(k_{2} = \lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{y}{x} = 1,b_{2} = \lim_{x\to +\infty}(y - k_{2}x) = 0\) ，故 \(y = x\) 为斜渐近线.
综上所述,共4条渐近线.D正确.

### 第 3 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \( f(n) \) 表示方程 \( x e^{x} = n \) 的正实根 (\( x > 0 \)), \( n \) 为正整数, 则下列选项中正确的是

- **(A)** \(\{f(n)\}\) 收敛.
- **(B)** \(\left\{\frac{f(n)}{n}\right\}\) 发散.
- **(C)** \(\left\{\frac{f(n)\mathrm{e}^n}{n}\right\}\) 发散.
- **(D)** \(\left\{\frac{f(n)\mathrm{e}^n}{n}\right\}\) 收敛.

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

令 \(g(x) = x\mathrm{e}^x\) ，由已知 \(f(t)\) 是 \(g(x) = t\) 在 \([0, + \infty)\) 上的反函数.
当 \(x \geqslant 0\) 时, \(g'(x) = \mathrm{e}^x + x \mathrm{e}^x > 0\), 且 \(\lim_{x \to +\infty} g(x) = +\infty\), 故 \(f(t)\) 在 \([0, +\infty)\) 上严格单调递增, 从而 \(\lim_{t \to +\infty} f(t) = +\infty\), 故 \(\lim_{n \to \infty} f(n) = +\infty\), \(\{f(n)\}\) 发散.
又由 \(\frac{f(n)}{n} = \frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^{f(n)}}\rightarrow 0(n\rightarrow \infty)\) ，知 \(\left\{\frac{f(n)}{n}\right\}\) 收敛，由反函数求导法则，有
\[
f ^ {\prime} (t) = \frac {1}{g ^ {\prime} (x)} \Bigg | _ {x = f (t)} = \frac {1}{\mathrm{e} ^ {x} + x \mathrm{e} ^ {x}} \Bigg | _ {x = f (t)} = \frac {1}{\mathrm{e} ^ {f (t)} + f (t) \mathrm{e} ^ {f (t)}}
\]
\[
\begin{array}{l} \lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} \frac {f (n) \mathrm{e} ^ {n}}{n} = \lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {n}}{\mathrm{e} ^ {f (n)}} = \lim _ {t \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {t}}{\mathrm{e} ^ {f (t)}} \xlongequal {\text {洛必达}} \lim _ {t \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {t}}{\mathrm{e} ^ {f (t)} \cdot f ^ {\prime} (t)} = \lim _ {t \rightarrow + \infty} \mathrm{e} ^ {t} \cdot \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {f (t)} + f (t) \mathrm{e} ^ {f (t)}}{\mathrm{e} ^ {f (t)}} \\ = \lim _ {t \rightarrow + \infty} \mathrm{e} ^ {t} [ 1 + f (t) ] = + \infty \\ \end{array}
\]
故 \(\left\{\frac{f(n)\mathrm{e}^n}{n}\right\}\) 发散. C 正确.

</details>

### 第 4 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \( f(x) \) 在 \((- \infty, + \infty)\) 内可导, 下列结论正确的是

- **(A)** 若 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f(x) = a\) ，则 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f'(x) = 0.\)
- **(B)** 若 \(\lim_{x\to -\infty}f'(x) = -\infty\) ，则 \(\lim_{x\to -\infty}f(x) = -\infty .\)
- **(C)** 若 \(\lim_{x\to -\infty}f(x) = -\infty\) ，则 \(\lim_{x\to -\infty}f'(x) = -\infty .\)
- **(D)** 若 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f'(x) = +\infty\) ，则 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f(x) = +\infty .\)

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

对于选项 A, 若  \( \lim_{x\to+\infty}f(x)=a,\lim_{x\to+\infty}f'(x) \)  可能不存在.
如 \(f(x) = \frac{\sin x^3}{1 + x^2}, \lim_{x\to +\infty}f(x) = 0\)，但 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f'(x) = \lim_{x\to +\infty}\left[-\frac{2x\sin x^3}{(1 + x^2)^2} +\frac{3x^2}{1 + x^2}\cos x^3\right]\) 不存在. 排除选项A.
对于选项 B, 如  \( f(x)=x^{2} \) ,  \( \lim_{x\to-\infty}f'(x)=-\infty \) , 但  \( \lim_{x\to-\infty}f(x)=+\infty \) . 排除选项 B.
对于选项 C, 若  \( \lim_{x\to-\infty}f(x)=-\infty \) , 则  \( \lim_{x\to-\infty}f'(x)=-\infty \)  不成立.
如 \(f(x) = x, \lim_{x \to -\infty} f(x) = -\infty\)，但 \(\lim_{x \to -\infty} f'(x) = 1\). 排除选项 C.
只有选项 D 正确. 事实上, 由 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f'(x) = +\infty\), 知存在 \(X > 0\), 当 \(x > X\) 时, \(f'(x) > 1\). 由拉格朗日中值定理, 知
\[
f (x) = f (X) + f ^ {\prime} (\xi) (x - X) \quad (x > X, \xi \in (X, x))
\]
故 \(f(x) \geqslant f(X) + (x - X)(x > X)\), 从而 \(\lim_{x \to +\infty} f(x) = +\infty\).

### 第 7 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设有矩阵 \(A_{n \times s}, B_{s \times n}\), 且 \(r(A) = s, r(B) = n, E_{n \times n}\) 为单位矩阵, 则下列齐次线性方程组有非零解的是

- **(A)** \(\left[ \begin{array}{cc}AB & O\\ E & A \end{array} \right]X = 0.\)
- **(B)** \(\left[ \begin{array}{cc}AB & O\\ E & B \end{array} \right]X = 0.\)
- **(C)** \(\left[ \begin{array}{cc}A & E\\ O & AB \end{array} \right]X = 0.\)
- **(D)** \(\left[ \begin{array}{cc}A & AB\\ E & B \end{array} \right]X = 0.\)

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

由 \(A_{n \times s}\) 及 \(\mathbf{r}(\mathbf{A}) = s\), 可知 \(n \geqslant s\); 由 \(B_{s \times n}\) 及 \(\mathbf{r}(\mathbf{B}) = n\), 可知 \(s \geqslant n\), 故 \(s = n\). 所以 \(\mathbf{A}\), \(\mathbf{B}, \mathbf{AB}\) 均为 \(n\) 阶可逆矩阵. 所以有
\[
\mathrm{r} \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \boldsymbol {A B} & \boldsymbol {O} \\ \boldsymbol {E} & \boldsymbol {A} \end{array} \right] = \mathrm{r} (\boldsymbol {A B}) + \mathrm{r} (\boldsymbol {A}) = n + n = 2 n
\]
\[
\mathrm{r} \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \boldsymbol {A B} & \boldsymbol {O} \\ \boldsymbol {E} & \boldsymbol {B} \end{array} \right] = \mathrm{r} (\boldsymbol {A B}) + \mathrm{r} (\boldsymbol {B}) = n + n = 2 n
\]
\[
\mathrm{r} \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \boldsymbol {A} & \boldsymbol {E} \\ \boldsymbol {O} & \boldsymbol {A B} \end{array} \right] = \mathrm{r} (\boldsymbol {A}) + \mathrm{r} (\boldsymbol {A B}) = n + n = 2 n
\]
因初等变换不改变矩阵的秩,所以
\[
\mathrm{r} \left[ \begin{array}{l l} \mathbf {A} & \mathbf {A B} \\ \mathbf {E} & \mathbf {B} \end{array} \right] = \mathrm{r} \left[ \begin{array}{l l} \mathbf {O} & \mathbf {O} \\ \mathbf {E} & \mathbf {B} \end{array} \right] = \mathrm{r} \left[ \begin{array}{l l} \mathbf {O} & \mathbf {O} \\ \mathbf {E} & \mathbf {O} \end{array} \right] = n <   2 n
\]
故选项 D 中的方程组有非零解.
D 正确.

= \lambda^ {3} \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda & - 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda & - 1 \\ 0 & 0 & \lambda \end{array} \right] ^ {- 1} = \lambda^ {3} \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \frac {1}{\lambda} & \frac {1}{\lambda^ {2}} & \frac {1}{\lambda^ {3}} \\ 0 & \frac {1}{\lambda} & \frac {1}{\lambda^ {2}} \\ 0 & 0 & \frac {1}{\lambda} \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda^ {2} & \lambda & 1 \\ 0 & \lambda^ {2} & \lambda \\ 0 & 0 & \lambda^ {2} \end{array} \right]
\]
\[
\boldsymbol {P} ^ {- 1} (\boldsymbol {A} + \boldsymbol {A} ^ {*}) \boldsymbol {P} = \boldsymbol {P} ^ {- 1} \boldsymbol {A} \boldsymbol {P} + \boldsymbol {P} ^ {- 1} \boldsymbol {A} ^ {*} \boldsymbol {P} = \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda & - 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda & - 1 \\ 0 & 0 & \lambda \end{array} \right] + \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda^ {2} & \lambda & 1 \\ 0 & \lambda^ {2} & \lambda \\ 0 & 0 & \lambda^ {2} \end{array} \right]
\]
\[
= \left[ \begin{array}{c c c} \lambda + \lambda^ {2} & \lambda - 1 & 1 \\ 0 & \lambda + \lambda^ {2} & \lambda - 1 \\ 0 & 0 & \lambda + \lambda^ {2} \end{array} \right]
\]
故 \(|\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{A}^{*}| = (\lambda +\lambda^{2})^{3}\)

</details>

### 第 22 题 (解答题 · 12分)

设二维随机变量 \((X,Y)\) 的概率密度为 \(f(x,y) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{2}{\theta^2}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{2x + y}{\theta}}, & x > 0,y > 0,\\ 0, & \text{其他}, \end{array} \right.\) 其中 \(\theta >0\)
(I) 求 X 与 Y 的边缘概率密度  \( f_{X}(x) \)  与  \( f_{Y}(y) \) ;
(II) 求  \( E[\min(X,Y)] \) ;
(III) 若  \( X_{1}, X_{2}, \cdots, X_{n} \)  与  \( Y_{1}, Y_{2}, \cdots, Y_{m} \)  分别为来自总体 X 与总体 Y 的简单随机样本, 求  \( \theta \)  的最大似然估计量  \( \hat{\theta} \) , 并判断  \( \hat{\theta} \)  是否为  \( \theta \)  的无偏估计量.


<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

(I) 当 \(x \leqslant 0\) 时, \(f_{X}(x) = 0\);
当 \(x > 0\) 时，
\[
\begin{array}{l} f _ {X} (x) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} y = \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \frac {2}{\theta^ {2}} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {2 x + y}{\theta}} \mathrm{d} y = \frac {2}{\theta^ {2}} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {y}{\theta}} \int_ {0} ^ {+ \infty} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {y}{\theta}} \mathrm{d} y \\ = \frac {2}{\theta^ {2}} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {2 x}{\theta}} (- \theta) \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {y}{\theta}} \Bigg | _ {0} ^ {+ \infty} = \frac {2}{\theta} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {2}{\theta} x} \\ \end{array}
\]
故 \(f_{X}(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{2}{\theta}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{2}{\theta} x}, & x > 0\\ 0, & x\leqslant 0 \end{array} \right.\)
同理可求得
\[
f _ {Y} (y) = \int_ {- \infty} ^ {+ \infty} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {1}{\theta} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{\theta} y}, & y > 0 \\ 0, & y \leqslant 0 \end{array} \right.
\]
（Ⅱ）由（Ⅰ）知， \( f_{X}(x)\cdot f_{Y}(y)=f(x,y) \) ，故X与Y相互独立，且X服从参数为 \( \frac{2}{\theta} \) 的
指数分布, Y 服从参数为  \( \frac{1}{\theta} \)  的指数分布. 记  \( Z = \min(X, Y) \) , 则 Z 的分布函数为
\[
\begin{array}{l} F _ {Z} (z) = P \left\{\min (X, Y) \leqslant z \right\} = 1 - P \left\{\min (X, Y) > z \right\} \\ = 1 - P \{X > z, Y > z \} = 1 - P \{X > z \} \cdot P \{Y > z \} \\ = 1 - [ 1 - P \{X \leqslant z \} ] [ 1 - P \{Y \leqslant z \} ] \\ = 1 - \left[ 1 - F _ {X} (z) \right] \left[ 1 - F _ {Y} (z) \right] \\ = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 1 - \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {3}{\theta} z}, & z > 0 \\ 0, & z \leqslant 0 \end{array} \right. \\ \end{array}
\]
即 \(Z = \min (X,Y)\) 服从参数为 \(\frac{3}{\theta}\) 的指数分布. 故
\[
E \left[ \min (X, Y) \right] = \frac {1}{\frac {3}{\theta}} = \frac {\theta}{3}
\]
(Ⅲ) \(\theta\) 的似然函数为
\[
L (\theta) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \left(\frac {2}{\theta}\right) ^ {n} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {2}{\theta} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} x _ {i}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{\theta}\right) ^ {m} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{\theta} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} y _ {i}}, & x _ {i} > 0, y _ {i} > 0 \\ 0, & \text {其他} \end{array} \right.
\]
\[
= \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {2 ^ {n}}{\theta^ {n + m}} \mathrm{e} ^ {- \left(\frac {2}{\theta} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} x _ {i} + \frac {1}{\theta} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} y _ {i}\right)}, & x _ {i} > 0, y _ {i} > 0 \\ 0, & \text {其他} \end{array} \right.
\]
取对数,得
\[
\ln L (\theta) = n \ln 2 - (n + m) \ln \theta - \left(\frac {2}{\theta} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} x _ {i} + \frac {1}{\theta} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} y _ {i}\right)
\]
令 \(\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}\theta}\ln L(\theta) = -\frac{n + m}{\theta} +\frac{2}{\theta^2}\sum_{i = 1}^{n}x_i + \frac{1}{\theta^2}\sum_{i = 1}^{m}y_i = 0\) ，解得 \(\theta = \frac{2\sum_{i = 1}^{n}x_i + \sum_{i = 1}^{m}y_i}{n + m}\) 故 \(\theta\) 的最大似然估计量为
\[
\hat {\theta} = \frac {2 \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} X _ {i} + \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} Y _ {i}}{n + m}
\]
又 \(EX = \frac{\theta}{2}, EY = \theta\) ，故
\[
E \hat {\theta} = \frac {1}{n + m} \left(2 \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} E X _ {i} + \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m} E Y _ {i}\right) = \frac {1}{n + m} \left(2 \cdot \frac {\theta}{2} \cdot n + \theta \cdot m\right) = \theta
\]
故 \(\hat{\theta}\) 是 \(\theta\) 的无偏估计量.

</details>
