### 第 1 题 (选择题 · 5分)

\( f(x,y)=x^{4}+y^{4}-(x+y)^{2} \) 有

- **(A)** 2 个极小值点, 1 个极大值点.
- **(B)** 1 个极小值点, 1 个极大值点.
- **(C)** 3 个极小值点,无极大值点.
- **(D)** 2 个极小值点,无极大值点.

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

令
\[
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} f _ {x} ^ {\prime} = 4 x ^ {3} - 2 x - 2 y = 0, \\ f _ {y} ^ {\prime} = 4 y ^ {3} - 2 x - 2 y = 0, \end{array} \right.
\]
得到函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的3个驻点 \( (0,0),(1,1),(-1,-1) \) .求二阶偏导数,得
\[
f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) = 1 2 x ^ {2} - 2, f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) = - 2, f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (x, y) = 1 2 y ^ {2} - 2.
\]
在点(1,1)处，
\[
A = f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) = 1 0, B = f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) = - 2, C = f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (1, 1) = 1 0,
\]
则 \(AC - B^{2} > 0, A > 0\)
故(1,1)是函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的极小值点；
在点 \( (-1,-1) \) 处，
\[
A = f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (- 1, - 1) = 1 0, B = f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (- 1, - 1) = - 2, C = f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (- 1, - 1) = 1 0,
\]
则  \( AC - B^{2} > 0, A > 0, \)
故 \( (-1,-1) \) 是函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的极小值点；
在点 \( (0,0) \) 处，
\[
A = f _ {x x} ^ {\prime \prime} (0, 0) = - 2, B = f _ {x y} ^ {\prime \prime} (0, 0) = - 2, C = f _ {y y} ^ {\prime \prime} (0, 0) = - 2,
\]
则 \(AC - B^{2} = 0\)
无法使用充分条件判断.对于 \(0 < |x| < 1\) ，有
\[
f (x, 0) = x ^ {4} - x ^ {2} <   0, f (x, - x) = 2 x ^ {4} > 0, f (0, 0) = 0,
\]
故 \( (0,0) \) 不是函数 \( f(x,y) \) 的极值点.故选D.

</details>

### 第 2 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设函数 \( f(x, y) \) 连续，且 \( f(x, y) = f(y, x) = f(-x, y) \)，则 \( \int_{-2}^{2} dx \int_{\sqrt{4 - x^2}}^{2} f(x, y) \, \mathrm{d}y = \)

- **(A)** \(\int_0^2\left[\int_{-2}^{-\sqrt{4 - y^2}}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x + \int_{-\sqrt{4 - y^2}}^{2}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x\right]\mathrm{d}y.\)
- **(B)** \(2\int_{0}^{2}\left[\int_{\sqrt{4 - y^{2}}}^{2}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}y\right]\mathrm{d}x.\)
- **(C)** \(4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}d\theta \int_{2}^{2\sec \theta}f(r\cos \theta ,r\sin \theta)rdr.\)
- **(D)** \(4\int_{0}^{\arctan \frac{1}{2}}d\theta \int_{2}^{2\sec \theta}f(r\cos \theta ,r\sin \theta)rdr.\)

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

由题意知,此二重积分的积分区域为
\[
D = \{(x, y) | \sqrt {4 - x ^ {2}} \leqslant y \leqslant 2, - 2 \leqslant x \leqslant 2 \},
\]
对应图像如图所示.
![](/uploads/books/94e1b1e059b70f301a1cb9d33ba978457dfdb95200564299c5d88c6a14bf4089.jpg)
参考答案与分析 卷(六)
\[
\begin{array}{l} I = \int_ {- 2} ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x \int_ {\sqrt {4 - x ^ {2}}} ^ {2} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} y \\ = \int_ {0} ^ {2} \left[ \int_ {- 2} ^ {- \sqrt {4 - y ^ {2}}} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {\sqrt {4 - y ^ {2}}} ^ {2} f (x, y) \mathrm{d} x \right] \mathrm{d} y. \\ \end{array}
\]
故选项A错误.
又 \( f(x,y)=f(-x,y) \) ，且积分区域关于y轴对称，则 \( I=2\int_{0}^{2}\left[\int_{\sqrt{4-y^{2}}}^{2}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x\right]\mathrm{d}y \) 。故选项B错误。
记 \(D_{1} = \{(x,y)\mid (x,y)\in D,x > y\}\) .由 \(f(x,y) = f(y,x)\) ，得 \(I = 4\iint_{D_1}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y.\)
用极坐标来表示， \( I=4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{2}^{2\sec\theta}f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)r\mathrm{d}r. \)  故选C.

</details>

### 第 3 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(f(x)\) 在 \((0, +\infty)\) 内可导, \(a\) 为常数. 对于以下结论:
①若 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f''(x) = a\) ，则 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{f(x)}{x} = a;\)
②若 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{f(x)}{x} = a\) ，则 \(\lim_{x\to +\infty}f''(x) = a.\)
下列说法中正确的是

- **(A)** ①正确, ②错误.
- **(B)** ①错误, ②正确.
- **(C)** ①与②均正确.
- **(D)** ①与②均错误.

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

对于①,严格来讲,要用定义法证明.
若 \(a = 0\) ，则对任意的 \(\varepsilon >0\) ，存在 \(X > 0\) ，当 \(x > X\) 时， \(\left|f^{\prime}(x)\right| <   \frac{\varepsilon}{2}\)
故结合拉格朗日中值定理, 存在  \( \xi \in (X, x) \) , 有
\[
\begin{array}{l} \left| \frac {f (x)}{x} \right| \leqslant \frac {1}{| x |} [ | f (x) - f (X) | + | f (X) | ] = \frac {| x - X |}{| x |} | f ^ {\prime} (\xi) | + \frac {| f (X) |}{| x |} \\ = \left(1 - \frac {X}{x}\right) | f ^ {\prime} (\xi) | + \frac {| f (X) |}{x} <   1 \cdot \frac {\varepsilon}{2} + \frac {| f (X) |}{x}, \\ \end{array}
\]
当 \( f(X)=0 \) 时，有 \( \left|\frac{f(x)}{x}\right|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}<\varepsilon. \)
当 \( f(X)\neq0 \) 时，令 \( x_{0}=\max\left\{X,\frac{2|f(X)|}{\varepsilon}\right\} \) ，则当 \( x>x_{0} \) 时， \( \left|\frac{f(x)}{x}\right|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}+\frac{\varepsilon}{2}=\varepsilon \) .
若 \(a \neq 0\)，令 \(g(x) = f(x) - ax\)，同理可证，故①正确。
对于②, 可举反例, \(f(x) = \sin x\) 可予以否定, 选A.
【注】考试中,考生对①可用广义的洛必达法则,即  \( \lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\xlongequal{\infty}\lim_{x\to+\infty}\frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)} \)  来验证其成立,但该广义法则不在考试大纲的要求内.

</details>

### 第 4 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设函数 \( f(t) \) 连续, 令 \( F(x, y) = \int_{0}^{y - x} (y - x - t)f(t)dt \), 则

- **(A)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).
- **(B)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = -\frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).
- **(C)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).
- **(D)** \(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x} = -\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial x^2} = -\frac{\partial^2 F}{\partial y^2}\).

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\( F(x,y)=y\int_{0}^{y-x}f(t)\mathrm{d}t-x\int_{0}^{y-x}f(t)\mathrm{d}t-\int_{0}^{y-x}tf(t)\mathrm{d}t \)
则
\[
\frac {\partial F}{\partial x} = - y f (y - x) - \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t + x f (y - x) + (y - x) f (y - x) = - \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t,
\]
\[
\frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial x ^ {2}} = f (y - x),
\]
\[
\frac {\partial F}{\partial y} = \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t + y f (y - x) - x f (y - x) - (y - x) f (y - x) = \int_ {0} ^ {y - x} f (t) \mathrm{d} t,
\]
\[
\frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial y ^ {2}} = f (y - x),
\]
可得
\[
\frac {\partial F}{\partial x} = - \frac {\partial F}{\partial y}, \frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial x ^ {2}} = \frac {\partial^ {2} F}{\partial y ^ {2}}.
\]
故选C.

</details>

### 第 5 题 (选择题 · 5分)

曲线 \( y = \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x}\sqrt{x^2 - 4x + 1}} \) 的渐近线条数为

- **(A)** 1.
- **(B)** 2.
- **(C)** 3.
- **(D)** 4.

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(\lim_{x\to 0^{-}}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{1}{x}\sqrt{x^2 - 4x + 1}} = +\infty\) ，故 \(x = 0\) 为铅直渐近线.
\( \lim_{x\to\infty}e^{-\frac{1}{x}\sqrt{x^{2}-4x+1}}=+\infty \) ，故曲线无水平渐近线.
\[
\lim _ {x \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x} \sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1}}}{x} = \lim _ {x \rightarrow + \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x} \cdot x}}{x} = 1,
\]
\[
\begin{array}{l} \lim _ {x \rightarrow + \infty} \left(\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x} \sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1} - x}\right) = \lim _ {x \rightarrow + \infty} \left[ \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x} (\sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1} - x) + x} \left(\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x} - 1}\right)\right] \\ = \lim _ {x \rightarrow + \infty} e ^ {- \frac {1}{x}} \frac {- 4 x + 1}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1} + x} + \lim _ {x \rightarrow + \infty} x \left(- \frac {1}{x}\right) \\ = - 2 - 1 = - 3, \\ \end{array}
\]
故 \(y = x - 3\) 为曲线的一条斜渐近线.
\[
\lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x}} \sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1}}{x} = \lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x}} (- x)}{x} = - 1,
\]
\[
\lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} \left(\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x}} \sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1} + x\right) = \lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} \left[ \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x}} (\sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1} + x) - x \left(\mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1}{x}} - 1\right)\right]
\]
参考答案与分析 卷(六)
\[
\begin{array}{l} = \lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} e ^ {- \frac {1}{x}} \frac {- 4 x + 1}{\sqrt {x ^ {2} - 4 x + 1} - x} - \lim _ {x \rightarrow - \infty} x \left(- \frac {1}{x}\right) \\ = 2 - (- 1) = 3. \\ \end{array}
\]
故 \( y = -x + 3 \) 为曲线的一条斜渐近线.
综上, 曲线共有3条渐近线, 故选C.

</details>

### 第 6 题 (选择题 · 5分)

直角三角板的斜边长度为 1, 将该直角三角板竖直插入水中, 并使其中一个直角边与水面对齐, 当三角板受到水的压力最大时, 其水平直角边长度为

- **(A)** \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\).
- **(B)** \(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\).
- **(C)** \(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\).
- **(D)** \(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\).

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

设三角形斜边与水面夹角为  \( \theta \)  (见图), 则斜边所在直线的方程为
\[
y = \cos \theta - x \cdot \cot \theta .
\]
直角三角板所受水的压力为
\[
\begin{array}{l} P = \rho g \int_ {0} ^ {\sin \theta} x (\cos \theta - x \cdot \cot \theta) d x \\ = \rho g \left(\cos \theta \int_ {0} ^ {\sin \theta} x d x - \cot \theta \int_ {0} ^ {\sin \theta} x ^ {2} d x\right) \\ = \frac {1}{6} \rho g \cos \theta \sin^ {2} \theta , \\ \end{array}
\]
![](/uploads/books/55b0548c983bf68cf5f9b500bc70ebe47fe23391a660a592a5cf164faad84b1d.jpg)
其中  \( \rho \)  为水的密度, g 为重力加速度.
\[
P ^ {\prime} (\theta) = \frac {1}{6} \rho g (2 \sin \theta \cos^ {2} \theta - \sin^ {3} \theta),
\]
令 \(P^{\prime}(\theta) = 0\) 得到唯一驻点 \(\theta = \arccos \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\)
于是当 \(\theta = \arccos \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\)，即水平直角边长度 \((\cos \theta) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\) 时，三角板受到水的压力最大，故选A.

</details>

### 第 7 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(D\) 为由 \(x = 0, y = 0, x + y = \frac{1}{2}, x + y = 1\) 所围成的区域, \(I_{1} = \iint_{D} \ln^{7}(x + y) d\sigma, I_{2} = \iint_{D} (x + y)^{7} d\sigma\), \(I_{3} = \iint_{D} \sin^{7}(x + y) d\sigma\), 则

- **(A)** \(I_{1} <   I_{2} <   I_{3}\)
- **(B)** \(I_{1} <   I_{3} <   I_{2}\)
- **(C)** \(I_{3} <   I_{2} <   I_{1}\)
- **(D)** \(I_{3} <   I_{1} <   I_{2}\)

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>

### 第 8 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(A, B\) 是 \(n\) 阶矩阵, \(A^{*}, B^{*}\) 分别是 \(A, B\) 对应的伴随矩阵, 则分块矩阵 \(C = \begin{pmatrix} O & A \\ B & O \end{pmatrix}\) 的伴随矩阵 \(C' =\)

- **(B)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}O & (-1)^{n}|A|B^{*}\\ (-1)^{n}|B|A^{*} & O \end{array} \right).\)
- **(C)** \(\left( \begin{array}{cc}O & |B|A^{*}\\ |A|B^{*} & O \end{array} \right).\)

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

代入选项逐个验算即可,其中
\[
\begin{array}{l} \left( \begin{array}{c c} O & A \\ B & O \end{array} \right) \left( \begin{array}{c c} O & (- 1) ^ {n} | A | B ^ {*} \\ (- 1) ^ {n} | B | A ^ {*} & O \end{array} \right) \\ = \left( \begin{array}{c c} (- 1) ^ {n} | B | A A ^ {*} & O \\ O & (- 1) ^ {n} | A | B B ^ {*} \end{array} \right) \\ \end{array}

\[
= (- 1) ^ {n} | A | | B | \left( \begin{array}{c c} E & O \\ O & E \end{array} \right) = | C | \left( \begin{array}{c c} E & O \\ O & E \end{array} \right) = C C ^ {*}.
\]
故选B.

### 第 11 题 (填空题 · 5分)

设 \(x \to 0\) 时, \(\ln \frac{2 + x}{2 + \sin x}\) 与 \(x^n\) 为同阶无穷小, 则 \(n =\) ____.

> **标准答案**：`3`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

当 \(x \to 0\) 时，
\[
\ln \frac {2 + x}{2 + \sin x} = \ln \frac {2 + \sin x - \sin x + x}{2 + \sin x} \sim \frac {- \sin x + x}{2 + \sin x} \sim \frac {1}{2} \left(\frac {1}{6} x ^ {3}\right) = \frac {1}{1 2} x ^ {3}.
\]
故与 \( x^{3} \) 同阶，n=3。

</details>

### 第 12 题 (填空题 · 5分)

使得 \(\ln x \leqslant a\sqrt{x}\) 成立的最小正数 \(a\) 为 ____.

> **标准答案**：`\(\frac{2}{\mathrm{e}}\)`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

设 \( f(x)=\frac{\ln x}{\sqrt{x}} \) ，转化为求 \( f(x) \) 在x>0时的最大值.于是，令 \( f'(x)=\frac{2-\ln x}{\frac{3}{2x^{2}}}=0 \) ，得 \( x=e^{2} \) .
