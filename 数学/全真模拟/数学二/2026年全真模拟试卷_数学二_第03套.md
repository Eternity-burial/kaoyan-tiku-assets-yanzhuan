### 第 1 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \( f(x) = \int_{0}^{x} (x - 2t) \mathrm{e}^{-t^2} dt (x \geqslant 0), g(x) = \int_{0}^{x} (x - 2t) \sin t^2 dt \)，则

- **(A)** \(f(x)\) 单调增加, \(g(x)\) 是奇函数.
- **(B)** \(f(x)\) 单调增加, \(g(x)\) 是偶函数.
- **(C)** \(f(x)\) 单调减少, \(g(x)\) 是奇函数.
- **(D)** \(f(x)\) 单调减少, \(g(x)\) 是偶函数.

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

\(f(x) = \int_{0}^{x}(x - 2t)\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t = x\int_{0}^{x}\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t - \int_{0}^{x}2t\mathrm{e}^{-t^2}\mathrm{d}t,\)
求导得
\[
f ^ {\prime} (x) = \int_ {0} ^ {x} \mathrm{e} ^ {- t ^ {2}} \mathrm{d} t + x \mathrm{e} ^ {- t ^ {2}} - 2 x \mathrm{e} ^ {- x ^ {2}} = \int_ {0} ^ {x} \mathrm{e} ^ {- t ^ {2}} \mathrm{d} t - x \mathrm{e} ^ {- x ^ {2}},
\]
易知 \( f'(0)=0 \) .
又 \(f''(x) = \mathrm{e}^{-x^2} - (\mathrm{e}^{-x^2} - 2x^2\mathrm{e}^{-x^2}) = 2x^2\mathrm{e}^{-x^2}\geqslant 0\) ，故 \(f^{\prime}(x)\) 单调增加，进而有 \(f^{\prime}(x)\geqslant f^{\prime}(0) = 0\) 则 \(f(x)\) 单调增加.
\[
g (x) = \int_ {0} ^ {x} (x - 2 t) \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t = x \int_ {0} ^ {x} \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t - \int_ {0} ^ {x} 2 t \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t,
\]
由于 \(\int_0^x\sin t^2\mathrm{d}t\) 为奇函数， \(\int_0^x 2t\sin t^2\mathrm{d}t\) 为偶函数，故 \(g(x)\) 为偶函数.
【注】对 \(g(x)\) 亦可求导，得
\[
g ^ {\prime} (x) = x \sin x ^ {2} + \int_ {0} ^ {x} \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t - 2 x \sin x ^ {2} = \int_ {0} ^ {x} \sin t ^ {2} \mathrm{d} t - x \sin x ^ {2},
\]
\[
g ^ {\prime \prime} (x) = \sin x ^ {2} - (\sin x ^ {2} + 2 x ^ {2} \cos x ^ {2}) = - 2 x ^ {2} \cos x ^ {2}.
\]
因为  \( g''(x) \)  为偶函数, 且  \( g'(0)=0 \) , 故  \( g'(x) \)  为奇函数, 进而可知  \( g(x) \)  为偶函数.

</details>

### 第 2 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设函数 \(f(x)\) 在 \([0,1]\) 上二阶可导, 则

- **(A)** 当 \( f'(x) < 0 \) 时, \( f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) < \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x \).
- **(B)** 当 \( f'(x) > 0 \) 时, \( f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) < \int_{0}^{1} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x \).
- **(C)** 当 \( f''(x) < 0 \) 时, \( f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right) < \int_{0}^{1} f(x^{2}) \, \mathrm{d}x \).
- **(D)** 当 \( f''(x) > 0 \) 时, \( f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right) < \int_{0}^{1} f(x^{2}) \, \mathrm{d}x \).

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

将 \( f(t) \) 在 \( t = \frac{1}{3} \) 处展开, 得
\[
f (t) = f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) + f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) \left(t - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \frac {f ^ {\prime \prime} (\xi)}{2} \left(t - \frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2},
\]
\(\xi\) 介于 \(t\) 与 \(\frac{1}{3}\) 之间.
当 \( f''(t)>0 \) 时， \( f''(\xi)>0,\frac{f''(\xi)}{2}\left(t-\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}\geqslant0 \) ，故
\[
f (t) \geqslant f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) + f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) \left(t - \frac {1}{3}\right).
\]
于是 \(f(t^2) \geqslant f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right) + f'\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)\left(t^2 - \frac{1}{3}\right)\)，当且仅当 \(t^2 = \frac{1}{3}\) 时，等号成立，则
\[
\int_ {0} ^ {1} f (t ^ {2}) \mathrm{d} t > \int_ {0} ^ {1} \left[ f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) + f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) \left(t ^ {2} - \frac {1}{3}\right) \right] \mathrm{d} t = f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) + \int_ {0} ^ {1} f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) \left(t ^ {2} - \frac {1}{3}\right) \mathrm{d} t = f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right),
\]
即 \(\int_0^1 f(x^2)\mathrm{d}x > f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right).\)
【注】由于 \(\int_0^1\left(t^2 -\frac{1}{3}\right)\mathrm{d}t = 0\) ，消除了 \(\int_0^1 f'\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)\left(t^2 -\frac{1}{3}\right)\mathrm{d}t\) 这一项．这是解决此题的关键.

</details>

### 第 3 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \( f'(x) > 0, f''(x) < 0, x > 0 \), 对任意的正整数 \( n \), 记
\[
I _ {1} = \frac {1}{2} [ f (n + 1) - f (n) ], \quad I _ {2} = f (n) - f \left(\frac {2 n - 1}{2}\right), I _ {3} = f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) - f (n),
\]
则

- **(A)** \(I_{1} > I_{2} > I_{3}\)
- **(B)** \(I_{1} > I_{3} > I_{2}\).
- **(C)** \(I_{2} > I_{1} > I_{3}\).
- **(D)** \(I_{2} > I_{3} > I_{1}\).

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

由拉格朗日中值定理, 得
\[
I _ {2} = f (n) - f \left(\frac {2 n - 1}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{2} f ^ {\prime} (\xi), \frac {2 n - 1}{2} <   \xi <   n,
\]
\[
I _ {3} = f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) - f (n) = \frac {1}{2} f ^ {\prime} (\eta), n <   \eta <   \frac {2 n + 1}{2}.
\]
由 \( f''(x) < 0 \)，知 \( f'(x) \) 单调减少，故 \( f'(\xi) > f'(\eta) \)，得 \( I_2 > I_3 \).
又
\[
I _ {1} = \frac {1}{2} \left[ f (n + 1) - f (n) \right] = \frac {1}{2} \left[ f (n + 1) - f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) \right] + \frac {1}{2} \left[ f \left(\frac {2 n + 1}{2}\right) - f (n) \right]
\]
\[
= \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\xi_ {1}) + \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\eta),
\]
其中， \(n < \eta < \frac{2n + 1}{2} < \xi_1 < n + 1\) ，故 \(f^{\prime}(\xi_1) < f^{\prime}(\eta)\) ，于是有
\[
I _ {1} <   \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\eta) + \frac {1}{4} f ^ {\prime} (\eta) = \frac {1}{2} f ^ {\prime} (\eta) = I _ {3}.
\]
综上， \( I_{2}>I_{3}>I_{1} \)

</details>

### 第 5 题 (选择题 · 5分)

边长为 1 的正方形闸门铅直放置于水中, 顶边与水面相平, 一条对角线将闸门分成两个三角形区域, 则两个三角形区域上所受水压力之比可以为

- **(A)** \(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\).
- **(B)** \(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\).

- **(D)** \(\frac{3}{2}\).

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

如图所示, 建立坐标系.
![](/uploads/books/9417f7605c1be224643847cb7b907c1747d73a0b332745dc52adb0b93ca5ad97.jpg)
设水的密度为 \(\rho\) .由图易知，对角线 \(AC\) 的方程为 \(x = -y + 1\)

### 第 6 题 (选择题 · 5分)

已知数列 \(\{x_{n}\}, \{y_{n}\}\), 且 \(x_{1} = y_{1} = k, 0 < k < 1, x_{n+1} = x_{n}^{2}, y_{n+1} = \sin y_{n} (n = 1, 2, \cdots)\), 则当 \(n \to \infty\) 时,

- **(A)** \(x_{n}\) 是 \(y_{n}\) 的高阶无穷小.
- **(B)** \(y_{n}\) 是 \(x_{n}\) 的高阶无穷小.
- **(C)** \(x_{n}\) 与 \(y_{n}\) 是等价无穷小.
- **(D)** \(x_{n}\) 与 \(y_{n}\) 是同阶但不等价的无穷小.

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

因为 \(x_{n} = x_{n - 1}^{2} = \dots = x_{1}^{2^{n - 1}} = k^{2^{n - 1}}\) ，所以 \(\lim_{n\to \infty}x_n = 0\)
因为 \(\sin x < x (x > 0)\) 且 \(0 < y_{1} < 1\)，所以 \(y_{n+1} = \sin y_n < y_n\)，故数列 \(\{y_n\}\) 单调减少。
又因为 \(\{y_n\} \subseteq (0, k]\)，即 \(\{y_n\}\) 有界，所以根据单调有界收敛定理，知 \(\lim_{n \to \infty} y_n\) 存在。
设 \(\lim_{n\to \infty}y_n = a\) ，在 \(y_{n + 1} = \sin y_n\) 两端令 \(n\to \infty\) ，则有 \(a = \sin a\) .所以 \(a = 0\) ，即 \(\lim_{n\to \infty}y_n = 0\)
设 \(z_{n} = \frac{x_{n}}{y_{n}}\) ，则
\[
\lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} \frac {z _ {n + 1}}{z _ {n}} = \lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} \left(\frac {x _ {n + 1}}{y _ {n + 1}} \cdot \frac {y _ {n}}{x _ {n}}\right) = \lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} \left(\frac {x _ {n} ^ {2}}{\sin y _ {n}} \cdot \frac {y _ {n}}{x _ {n}}\right) = \lim _ {n \rightarrow \infty} \left(x _ {n} \cdot \frac {y _ {n}}{\sin y _ {n}}\right) = 0,
\]
由收敛数列的保号性, 知存在正整数 \(N\), 当 \(n > N\) 时, \(\frac{z_{n+1}}{z_n} < \frac{1}{2}\), 于是
\[
0 <   z _ {n} <   \frac {1}{2} z _ {n - 1} <   \dots <   \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - N - 1} z _ {N + 1},
\]
所以 \(\lim_{n\to \infty}z_n = 0\) ，即 \(\lim_{n\to \infty}\frac{x_n}{y_n} = 0\) ， \(x_{n}\) 是 \(y_{n}\) 的高阶无穷小.

</details>

### 第 7 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \( f(1) = 1, f'(1) = 2 \)，则 \( \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{\ln f[\cos(x - 1) - \sin(x - 1)]}{\ln x} = \)

- **(A)** -2.
- **(B)** 2.
- **(C)** -e.
- **(D)** e.

> **标准答案**：`A`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

当 \(x \to 1\) 时, \(\ln x \sim x - 1\),
\[
\ln f \left[ \cos (x - 1) - \sin (x - 1) \right] \sim f \left[ \cos (x - 1) - \sin (x - 1) \right] - 1,
\]
则 原式 \(= \lim_{x\to 1}\frac{f[\cos(x - 1) - \sin(x - 1)] - 1}{x - 1}\)
\[
= \lim _ {x \rightarrow 1} \frac {f [ \cos (x - 1) - \sin (x - 1) ] - f (1)}{\cos (x - 1) - \sin (x - 1) - 1} \cdot \frac {\cos (x - 1) - \sin (x - 1) - 1}{x - 1}
\]
\[
= f ^ {\prime} (1) \cdot \lim _ {x \rightarrow 1} \left[ \frac {\cos (x - 1) - 1}{x - 1} - \frac {\sin (x - 1)}{x - 1} \right]
\]
\[
= - f ^ {\prime} (1) = - 2.
\]

</details>

### 第 8 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设 \(n\) 阶矩阵 \(A, B, C\) 满足 \(r(ABC) + 2n = r(A) + r(B) + r(C)\), 则

- **(A)** \(r(A) + r(B) + r(C) = r(AB)\).
- **(B)** \(r(AB) + n = r(A) + r(B)\).
- **(C)** \(r(ABC) > r(A) + r(BC) - n.\)
- **(D)** \(r(AB) = r(BC) = n.\)

> **标准答案**：`B`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

因 \(r(ABC) \geqslant r(AB) + r(C) - n\)，则
\[
r (A) + r (B) + r (C) = r (A B C) + 2 n \geqslant r (A B) + r (C) - n + 2 n,
\]
即 \(r(A) + r(B) \geqslant r(AB) + n\).
又 \(r(AB) \geqslant r(A) + r(B) - n\)，即 \(r(AB) + n \geqslant r(A) + r(B)\).
综上， \( r(AB)+n=r(A)+r(B) \) . 故选项B正确.
同理,选项C不成立.
若 \(r(A) + r(B) + r(C) = r(AB)\)，则有 \(r(AB) = r(ABC) + 2n \geqslant 2n\)，矛盾，故选项A不成立。
显然,选项D不一定成立.
【注】本题亦可举反例，令 A=B=E, C=O，可排除选项 A, C, D.

</details>

### 第 9 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设矩阵  \( \begin{pmatrix}1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & a & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & b\end{pmatrix} \)  有 2 个正特征值和 1 个负特征值，则

- **(A)** \(a > 4, b > 1\).
- **(B)** \(a < 4, b > 1\).
- **(C)** \((a - 4)b > 1\).
- **(D)** \((a - 4)b < 1\).

> **标准答案**：`D`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

记矩阵 \(A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 2 & a & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & b \end{pmatrix}\), 其为实对称矩阵, 则对应的二次型为
\[
\begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = x _ {1} ^ {2} + a x _ {2} ^ {2} + b x _ {3} ^ {2} + 4 x _ {1} x _ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} \\ = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + (a - 4) x _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} + b x _ {3} ^ {2}. \\ \end{array}
\]
①当 \(a = 4, b \neq 0\) 时，
\[
\begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + b x _ {3} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} \\ = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + b \left(x _ {3} + \frac {1}{b} x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} - \frac {1}{b} x _ {2} ^ {2}, \\ \end{array}
\]
此时满足矩阵 A 的特征值两正一负的条件.
②当 \(a = 4\) ， \(b = 0\) 时，
\[
f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3}.
\]
令 \(\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{1} + 2x_{2} = y_{1}, \\ x_{2} = y_{2} + y_{3}, \end{array} \right.\) 得 \(f = y_{1}^{2} + 2y_{2}^{2} - 2y_{3}^{2}\)，此时满足矩阵 \(A\) 的特征值两正一负的条件.
\(x_{3} = y_{2} - y_{3}\)
③当 \( a \neq 4 \) 时，
\[
\begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}, x _ {2}, x _ {3}\right) = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + (a - 4) x _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} x _ {3} + b x _ {3} ^ {2} \\ = \left(x _ {1} + 2 x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + (a - 4) \left(x _ {2} + \frac {1}{a - 4} x _ {3}\right) ^ {2} + \left(b - \frac {1}{a - 4}\right) x _ {3} ^ {2}. \\ \end{array}
\]
要满足矩阵 A 的特征值两正一负, 则有  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}a-4&>0,\\ b-\frac{1}{a-4}&<0\end{aligned}\right. \)  或  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}a-4&<0,\\ b-\frac{1}{a-4}&>0,\end{aligned}\right. \)  此时均有  \( (a-4)b<1 \) .
综上， \((a - 4)b < 1\)

</details>

### 第 10 题 (选择题 · 5分)

设向量组 \(B: \beta_{1}, \beta_{2}, \cdots, \beta_{r}\) 能由向量组 \(A: \alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \cdots, \alpha_{s}\) 线性表示为 \(\left( \begin{array}{c} \beta_{1} \\ \beta_{2} \\ \vdots \\ \beta_{r} \end{array} \right) = P \left( \begin{array}{c} \alpha_{1} \\ \alpha_{2} \\ \vdots \\ \alpha_{s} \end{array} \right)\), 其中 \(P\) 为 \(r \times s\) 矩
阵, 且向量组 \(A\) 线性无关, 则向量组 \(B\) 线性无关是 \(r(P) = r\) 的

- **(A)** 充分非必要条件.
- **(B)** 必要非充分条件.
- **(C)** 充分必要条件.
- **(D)** 既非充分也非必要条件.

> **标准答案**：`C`  

<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

必要性.用反证法证明.
假设  \( \beta_{1},\beta_{2},\cdots,\beta_{r} \)  线性相关，则存在不全为零的数  \( k_{1},k_{2},\cdots,k_{r} \) ，使
\[
k _ {1} \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {1} + k _ {2} \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {2} + \dots + k _ {r} \boldsymbol {\beta} _ {r} = \mathbf {0},
\]
则  \( (k_{1}, k_{2}, \cdots, k_{r}) \begin{pmatrix} \boldsymbol{\beta}_{1} \\ \boldsymbol{\beta}_{2} \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol{\beta}_{r} \end{pmatrix} = \mathbf{0} \) ,
即  \( (k_{1}, k_{2}, \cdots, k_{r})P\begin{pmatrix}\alpha_{1}\\ \alpha_{2}\\ \vdots\\ \alpha_{s}\end{pmatrix}=0 \) .
由于  \( \alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\cdots,\alpha_{s} \)  线性无关, 故有
\[
(k _ {1}, k _ {2}, \dots , k _ {r}) \boldsymbol {P} = \boldsymbol {0}.
\]
设 \( p_{1},p_{2},\cdots,p_{r} \) 是P的行向量组,则有
\[
(k _ {1}, k _ {2}, \dots , k _ {r}) \left( \begin{array}{c} \boldsymbol {p} _ {1} \\ \boldsymbol {p} _ {2} \\ \vdots \\ \boldsymbol {p} _ {r} \end{array} \right) = \mathbf {0},
\]
即 \(k_{1}\pmb{p}_{1} + k_{2}\pmb{p}_{2} + \dots +k_{r}\pmb{p}_{r} = \mathbf{0},\)

### 第 21 题 (解答题 · 12分)

设一光滑曲线在第一象限内且过坐标原点 O，曲线上任意一点 M 处的切线 MT 交 x 轴于点 T, MP ⊥ x 轴于点 P. 若三角形 MPT 的面积与曲边三角形 OMP 的面积之比恒为常数  \( k\left(k>\frac{1}{2}\right) \) ，且曲线是沿 x 轴正向单调增加的，求该曲线的方程.


<details>
<summary><b>点击查看深度推导与解析</b></summary>

**【解题推导】**：

设所求曲线的方程为 \( y = y(x) (x > 0, y > 0, y' > 0) \). 如图所示, 在曲线上任取一点 \( M(x, y) \), 则 \( PM = y(x) \), \( \frac{PM}{TP} = \tan \alpha = y'(x) \), 所以 \( TP = \frac{PM}{\tan \alpha} = \frac{y(x)}{y'(x)} \). 于是 \( \triangle MPT \) 的面积为 \( \frac{1}{2} PM \cdot TP = \frac{y^2(x)}{2y'(x)} \), 曲边三角形 OMP 的面积为 \( \int_0^x y(t) dt \).
![](/uploads/books/168c32a4c928ffa694dd441cd66f4dc2f557443a69cb94e067d8fa933c32edfa.jpg)
由题意得， \( \frac{\frac{y^{2}(x)}{2y'(x)}}{\int_{0}^{x}y'(t)\mathrm{d}t}=k \) ，即
\[
\frac {y ^ {2}}{y ^ {\prime}} = 2 k \int_ {0} ^ {x} y (t) \mathrm{d} t. \tag {①}
\]
……6分
方程①的两边同时对 \(x\) 求导, 得 \(y = y(x)\) 满足的微分方程为 \(\frac{y' \cdot 2yy' - y^2y''}{y'^2} = 2ky\), 即
\[
y y ^ {\prime \prime} + 2 (k - 1) y ^ {\prime 2} = 0, \tag {②}
\]
初始条件为 \( y(0)=0 \) .
令 \( y' = p \)，\( y'' = p\frac{\mathrm{d}p}{\mathrm{d}y} \)，则方程②化为 \( yp\frac{\mathrm{d}p}{\mathrm{d}y} = 2(1 - k)p^2 \)，即
\[
y \frac {\mathrm{d} p}{\mathrm{d} y} = 2 (1 - k) p. \tag {③}
\]
……8分
用分离变量法解方程③, 得通解为 \(p = C_{1}y^{2(1 - k)}\), 即
\[
y ^ {\prime} = C _ {1} y ^ {2 (1 - k)}. \tag {4}
\]
再用分离变量法解方程④, 得方程②的通解为
\[
\frac {1}{2 k - 1} y ^ {2 k - 1} = C _ {1} x + C _ {2},
\]
……10分
由 \(y(0) = 0\) 得 \(C_2 = 0\) ，又曲线是沿 \(x\) 轴正向单调增加的，故 \(C_1 > 0\) ，于是所求曲线的方程为\(y = Cx^{\frac{1}{2k - 1}}\) ， \(C\) 为正常数. ……12分
